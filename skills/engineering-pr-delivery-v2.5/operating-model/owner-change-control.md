# Owner change control

Owner-intent changes use Owner Decision Records (ODRs). An ODR captures exact decision/source, impact class, affected roadmap nodes/EPs/issues, required reconciliation and application state.

Typical impact classes: `LOCAL`, `EP`, `PHASE`, `ARCHITECTURE`, `PROGRAM`, `UX_CONCEPT`, `SCOPE`, `ENGINEERING_AUTHORITY`.

An ODR is not complete merely because it exists. Reconcile roadmap revision, stale EPs, issue graph, progress basis, frontier and REPO_STATE as applicable. Agents may capture Owner intent accurately; they may not enlarge it.
