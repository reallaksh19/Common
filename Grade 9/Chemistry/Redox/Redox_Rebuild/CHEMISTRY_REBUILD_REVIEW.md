# Chemistry rebuild review record

## Teaching grammar retained from the reference textbook snapshots

The rebuild uses a connected instructional rhythm rather than a dashboard of isolated cards:

`orient / familiar context → explain in ordinary language → meaningful chemistry representation → worked reasoning → rule / exception → misconception repair → guided/faded/independent practice → transfer`

The reference snapshots were treated as a structural benchmark, not a request to duplicate typography or copyrighted page design.

## Major repairs made during the build

- Chemistry typography moved to reliable subscripts/superscripts and explicit `e⁻` notation.
- Long title/subtitle zones were separated to prevent collision.
- Oxidation-number pages restored concept meaning before formula algebra.
- ST02 added a causal explanation for why electron loss makes oxidation number rise.
- ST03 added the SELF vs OTHER representation to repair the oxidising/reducing-agent inversion.
- ST04 separated reaction appearance/type from redox proof.
- ST05 added split/converge topology and later backfilled three eligible disproportionation PYQs found by the full-corpus audit.
- ST06 made “minimum justified path” an explicit exam skill rather than running every Redox step for every problem.

## What was intentionally not added

The supplied Redox source does not teach a full unit on:

- ion-electron / half-reaction balancing algorithms;
- titration, normality or equivalent weight;
- electrochemical potentials;
- medium-dependent reagent product prediction;
- salt/qualitative analysis;
- coordination chemistry;
- broad periodic/reactivity ranking.

ExamSIDE candidates whose minimum solution path depends on those topics are marked partial or out of scope rather than allowed to expand the chapter silently.
