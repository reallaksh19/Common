Review learner experience before audit/tooling.

## Review tracks

- [ ] Chemistry accuracy: oxidation numbers, electron direction, agent inversion, reaction classification and exceptions.
- [ ] Teaching/pedagogy: explanation order, meaningful representations, misconception repair and scaffold fading.
- [ ] Assessment/ExamSIDE: question fidelity, concept links, H1–H3 depth, Appendix A solutions and primary ownership.
- [ ] Source grounding/scope: supplied source obligations, QC notes, explicit external prerequisites and no silent topic expansion.
- [ ] Publication: formula typography, title/subtitle fit, readability at 100% zoom, page occupancy and print/digital use.
- [ ] Tooling/reproducibility: manifest/hash integrity, ledger counts, package validator and documented rebuild limitations.
- [ ] Owner acceptance before expansion to another Chemistry chapter.

## Approval checklist

A review track should not be marked approved merely because the current audit says PASS. Approval requires an independent reviewer to inspect the relevant learner-facing evidence.
