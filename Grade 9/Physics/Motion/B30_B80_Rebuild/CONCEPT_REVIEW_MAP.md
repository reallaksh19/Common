## Learning sequence and review probes

| Step | Prerequisite to make visible | Review probe |
|---|---|---|
| Position and direction | Origin, metre ticks, left/right, signed coordinate | Can the learner be right of zero while moving left? |
| Distance versus displacement | Ordered route, start/turn/finish, endpoint subtraction | Can the learner explain positive distance with zero displacement? |
| Constant-velocity area | Axes, elapsed duration, signed movement each second | Why is height × width in metres, and when is the rectangle rule valid? |
| Combining regions | Signed area, magnitudes, zero crossing | Can the learner split at a sign change before calculating distance? |
| Triangular region | Geometric half-rectangle; correct base/height | Can the learner justify the half and reject using a triangle for an arbitrary sloping segment? |

B30 builds one step before a complete task; B80 retains the decisive depiction and changes origin, sign or context. The profile is selected foundation content within a Grade 9–11 authoring range, not a board-specific syllabus certification. B90 minimum depiction is a skill rule; no B90 learner book is included.

For each finding record the concept ID, lesson/question ID, PDF page, learner consequence and proposed repair. Match claims and required representations across bands after changes. The independent tasks are original; they do not inherit the chapter’s 68-question source denominator.
