## Learning objective

Build Grade 9 / competitive-foundation understanding sufficient for the learner to explain and independently attack every concept represented by the source.

## Concept Book method

`SEE THE EQUATION -> REALIZE -> UNDERSTAND`

- **SEE** — equation inside a physical picture, graph, pattern, timeline, experiment, or thought experiment.
- **REALIZE** — every term acquires physical meaning and can be explained in ordinary language.
- **UNDERSTAND** — derive/reconstruct, state assumptions, check units/signs, connect graphs, reason proportionally, confront misconceptions, predict, and transfer.

`CONNECT` links each concept back to source question IDs and forward to the First-Step Book and Question Bank.
