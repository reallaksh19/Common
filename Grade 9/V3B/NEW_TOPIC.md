## Recovery and extensions

Read the canonical engineering chain and current topic packet, not old chat. Keep profile reuse, instance reuse and artifact reuse distinct. Independent review requires a different qualified instance. Compute transitive invalidation on a changed source/gate/baseline; reject stale work orders. Never delete old accepted evidence to conceal a change.

The Thermodynamics cycle binding demonstrates adding a subtopic without regenerating unrelated material. The Gravity overlay demonstrates research depth with explicit mathematical prerequisites and an advanced branch hold.
