# Assimilation and owner-directed practice

Status: PROPOSED PEDAGOGICAL CONTRACT. Research principles are sourced in [research](research.md); the operational choices here require topic-specific review.

## The owner brief is an independent input

PUR must preserve what the owner wants produced. A broad first-study/revision/exam label is useful but insufficient.

| Brief dimension | Examples | Consequence |
|---|---|---|
| Audience and current assumptions | Age/class, language, relevant concept/algebra/representation familiarity | Choose explanation and support; mark reported versus demonstrated readiness |
| Learning goal | Understand a difficult distinction; revisit a derivation; prepare for a named examination | Determines terminal content and assessment obligations |
| Practice goal | Very simplest recognition; routine application; supported multi-step; independent challenge; mixed set | Determines the actual Core2A experience |
| Target scope | Named subtopics, authorized syllabus, supplied corpus, selected extension | Sets the completeness denominator |
| Difficulty/support mix | All elementary; gradual progression; difficult questions with optional hints | Difficulty and support are independently specified |
| Quantity and budget | Item count, approximate study time, detail/page constraints | Prioritize explicitly; do not silently remove required foundations |
| Source policy | Source-only, preserve source bank plus generated practice, original new examples | Controls provenance and selection |
| Output policy | Worksheet/book, worked solutions location, hints optional, visual/accessibility needs | Controls realization and learner flow |
| Success definition | Material supports specified capabilities; requested question mix delivered | Does not assert learner mastery without learner evidence |
| Exclusions/overrides | No timed set, no advanced algebra, only elementary tasks today | Remain visible to all roles and versioned |

If a necessary choice is unspecified, make a reasonable stated proposal for a bounded draft. Ask the owner only when the ambiguity materially prevents a faithful product. Do not infer competitive practice merely because a source question is difficult.

LS records capability-specific assumptions. An overall 20/50/80 descriptor may be retained as reported context, but it cannot establish sign-convention knowledge, representation fluency or a particular inferential jump. Unknown capabilities receive conservative support or explicit optional bridges. Actual learner observations, if supplied, remain distinguishable from owner reports and author assumptions.

## The Core1A assimilation process

1. Identify the conceptual change: what currently plausible belief or missing connection prevents understanding?
2. Preserve Core1's valid reasoning spine and Core2's useful demand cues. List conditions and unresolved prerequisites.
3. Identify the mental operations required to make the connection. Choose meaningful teaching transitions and their justifications.
4. Specify what a visual, physical analogy, example or symbolic treatment needs to make perceptible.
5. Compare appropriate representations and decide their reading order. Explain how pictures/words/symbols refer to the same objects and relations.
6. Write the explanation, equation bridge and misconception contrast. Add a check that could reveal the target confusion.
7. Design worked/faded/independent opportunities at the support level the owner requests.
8. Recompose the pieces into a connected account of the whole concept.
9. Realize the manuscript and assets; inspect the actual content and layout.
10. Produce scoped T receipts only after review of the realized material.

This is an iterative design order, not a claim that one deterministic program can derive the optimum lesson. A representation may expose a missing prerequisite and send the plan back for revision.

## What makes an atom meaningful

An atom is a learning transition, not a sentence, heading, formula or minimum count. Splitting is a candidate response when the entity, model, frame, constraint, representation, algebraic rule or interpretation changes.

The proposed stop rule is learner-relative: the next transition uses one explicitly justified operation whose prerequisites have been supported or declared. This is a review heuristic, not a mathematically decidable guarantee. An algebraically single step may conceal several conceptual decisions. Conversely, mechanically separating every symbol replacement can obscure the relationship the learner needs to see.

For each transition record: entry assumption, learner action, why valid, intended interpretation and a check. Review whether the learner must make an unintroduced decision. When a transition is compressed, name the assumed capability. After decomposition, include a whole-problem explanation; otherwise atoms may produce disconnected fragments rather than assimilation.

Example: substituting a_y = -g into v_y = u_y + a_y t is a small algebraic move only after the learner understands signed components, constant acceleration and elapsed time. Those are separate pedagogical obligations, even if the written substitution takes one line.

## Equations and simplification

Simplify the route into an equation while preserving its conditions.

| Subject object | Core1 validity spine | Core1A teaching work |
|---|---|---|
| Physics law/model | Assumptions, frame, quantities, dimensions, derivation and limits | Connect physical event, chosen picture, signs and term meanings; explain a limit/counterexample |
| Mathematical definition/theorem/equivalence | Domains, hypotheses, proof or justification, reversibility and excluded values | Make each operation's meaning visible; connect numeric, symbolic and geometric cases; show a tempting invalid move |
| Chemical formula/equation/model | Species, composition, state/charge where relevant, conservation and model scope | Distinguish particles/amount/symbols; connect counts and coefficients; prevent treating stoichiometry as a mechanism |

Do not impose a Physics cause/law template on every mathematical identity or chemical equation. A simplified reaction picture is a model with stated limits. An analogy needs a mapping and a point where it stops applying.

## Representation contract

Each chosen representation states:
- what the learner should notice before doing the relevant mathematics;
- conventions needed to read it;
- which relation it makes visible;
- how labels and symbols connect to the prose/equation;
- what it must not imply;
- whether the published version actually satisfies these claims.

The owner explicitly wants pictorial and real-life support for difficult concepts. Apply that intention meaningfully, rather than satisfying a fixed image count. A familiar event can be an entry point, a diagram can clarify a relation, and a symbolic pattern can sometimes be the clearest first representation. Optional support can preserve detail for learners who need it.

Compare candidates against the named need. Numerical scoring without a calibrated rubric adds false precision. For example, an attractive trajectory picture may fail if it omits acceleration at the apex; a plain diagram with separate labelled vectors may directly resolve the confusion.

Inspect the rendered version: legibility, arrow origins/directions, axes, scale claims, symbol consistency, caption and nearby explanation. Alt text should express the relevant relationships, not merely say diagram.

## Difficulty, support and purpose

Keep at least these dimensions separate: conceptual novelty, number of dependencies, representation reading/switching, hidden conditions, inverse reasoning, algebra/computation, decision/solution-route choice and integration across subtopics. Then estimate the challenge relative to the explicit audience assumptions.

A supported competitive-style problem and an independent competitive problem can share content demands but impose different learner responsibilities. A very simple conceptual question may be more appropriate than a computational item if the owner wants confidence or one difficult distinction made secure.

Do not fix a universal progression rate from percentage. A draft may suggest a progression, record its assumptions and offer return links. It must preserve an owner-selected elementary-only request.

## Core2 intelligence must survive into Core2A

For each family retain the triggering cues, tempting wrong path, first useful decision, hidden conditions, required representations, alternate valid routes and transfer envelope:

- SAFE: variations that retain the same validated semantics and stated conditions.
- CONDITIONAL: require a named additional capability, representation or condition check.
- FORBIDDEN IN CURRENT SCOPE: break the model, change the mathematical domain, require untaught semantics or exceed an explicit owner constraint.

Safe does not mean automatically correct: every generated item still needs an independent solution and ambiguity check. Conditional eligibility can change when its prerequisite is taught and reviewed.

H1/H2/H3 describe staged support, not compulsory amounts of text. H1 may focus attention, H2 identify a useful relationship, H3 scaffold execution. Each carries typed semantic/operation dependencies. Do not use an apparently harmless H1 to introduce an unprovided law or give away the entire decisive step while labelling the item independent.

## Practice acceptance

Core2A must show that each selected question:
1. serves the owner brief;
2. is complete and unambiguous under stated conditions;
3. has a correct answer and reasoning, including alternate valid answers where applicable;
4. binds all semantic, hint and solution dependencies to reviewed support or an explicit diagnostic/stretch permission;
5. stays inside its declared transfer envelope;
6. carries honest source metadata and provenance;
7. offers the requested support without mislabelling independence.

Use separate provenance fields for item origin and the basis of its demand design. A newly written question inspired by an observed family is still newly authored; it is not an observed past-exam question. SYLLABUS_DERIVED requires an actual syllabus/source binding. PEDAGOGICALLY_GENERATED does not establish exam frequency.

## Product-level review

Review sequencing, navigation, optional hints and solution placement as a learning experience. A manuscript can contain every atom and still be confusing through poor order, excessive duplication or separation of a picture from its explanation.

Check that source-question preservation, owner-selected worksheet content and chapter completeness are each reconciled against their own inventory. Keep unsupported claims, deliberately deferred material and additional prerequisites visible. The architecture should be judged by these outcomes, not by the number of packets or agents.
