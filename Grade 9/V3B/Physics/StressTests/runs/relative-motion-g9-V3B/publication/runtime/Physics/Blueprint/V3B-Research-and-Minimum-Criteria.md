# V3B research freedom and minimum acceptance criteria

Owner-approved policy: production controls must preserve the web search, investigation and teaching exploration needed for good self-study material. This policy refines the existing blueprint; it does not replace its academic objectives or authorize changes to frozen Core1/Core2.

## Three working states

| State | Agent may do | Acceptance boundary |
|---|---|---|
| EXPLORATORY | Search the web, compare sources, investigate misconceptions, try explanations/derivations/figures, discover prerequisites and subtopics | Findings are provisional; no claim of verified science or learner readiness |
| REVIEW_DRAFT | Compose traceable candidate teaching and questions, including documented mathematical adaptations or results needing expert review | Minimum structural integrity; unresolved scientific claims visibly marked and linked to their review requirement |
| LEARNER_READY | Present the accepted teaching and practice for self-study | Applicable minimum criteria below satisfied, consequential scientific issues resolved, and required owner release authorization |

The first two states do not need an approved complete baseline before research or exploratory prose can begin. Start with a provisional scope and gate outline, improve them through investigation, and establish a versioned baseline for accepted production. A publication command's input restrictions are not permissions on what the author may research.

Search is performed by the authoring agent using its available web/research tools, not by the deterministic publication command. No search quota, fixed list of websites or approval request per search is introduced. Evaluate sources for the particular claim: prefer authoritative scientific/educational evidence for consequential claims, retain useful alternative explanations, compare conflicts and record limitations. A citation count alone cannot establish quality.

For Core1A/1B, Medium/Hard buckets still require research-informed enrichment. Explain what the research contributed to the difficult inference, representation or misconception. Easy follows the owner's no-enrichment-search rule; ordinary source reconciliation must still preserve supplied facts. Research depth and intrinsic difficulty remain separate from student knowledge percentage. Only Core2A/2B use knowledge evidence or an owner waiver for personalized routing.

## Minimum criteria for learner-ready content

| ID | Criterion | What must be demonstrated |
|---|---|---|
| MIN-1 | Scientific correctness | Consequential equations, data, assumptions, units, signs, model limits and diagram relationships checked by an appropriate method; uncertainty not concealed |
| MIN-2 | Required content coverage | Required concepts, equations, examples and fragile reasoning transitions are accounted for across products and realized where locally needed; no requirement silently removed |
| MIN-3 | Self-help closure | Every question/prompt has truthful provenance and an answer/model response/rubric, with sufficient explanation and help for its intended purpose |
| MIN-4 | Core and learner fit | Distinct A/B learner actions, intrinsic study-bucket depth, research contribution where required, and appropriate practice purpose/knowledge-or-waiver controls |
| MIN-5 | Usable publication | Readable equations and figures, correct problem/figure binding, accessible help/answers and no misleading presentation; final-medium inspection appropriate to the artifact |
| MIN-6 | Traceable accepted state | Original source identity retained; adaptations and accepted changes recorded; evidence bound to the correct content/version; affected stale evidence cannot authorize release |

These are substantive criteria, not counts of pages, hints, diagrams, fields or review receipts. The 17 detailed rules describe ways to establish the criteria; they are not 17 separate reasons to stop investigation. A short concept may be complete; a Hard bucket may need extensive decomposition. Neither length establishes depth by itself.

## Issue severity controls the effect

| Classification | Examples | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| BLOCKING_DEFECT | Known wrong numerical result, altered frozen question, missing required answer/content, broken provenance, unsafe/unrenderable publication object | Stop acceptance of the affected artifact; preserve rejected evidence and continue independent research/work |
| SCIENTIFIC_REVIEW_REQUIRED | Documented equation transformation, numerical family without a qualified evaluator, unresolved scientific interpretation | Permit a clearly labelled review draft; block learner-ready acceptance of the affected claim until reviewed |
| ADVISORY | Similarity score, cosmetic variation, page-count deviation, optional style improvement | Keep visible for triage; do not automatically block research, draft creation or release solely because of the flag |
| CAPABILITY_LIMIT | No renderer for a required original figure; absent browser or scientific evaluation tool | State what cannot yet be produced/verified; retain the source and research, hold only dependent acceptance, never manufacture PASS |

A reviewer may classify harmful repetition or a readability problem as a consequential defect. That decision must explain the learning consequence; the initial similarity/layout score does not decide it. Known wrong science remains a defect even if someone requests an expert-review route. A qualified existing oracle cannot be bypassed merely by asking for review.

## Flexible mathematics and self-help support

Exact source retention applies to originals; it does not require copying their equation notation into every Core. Authors may rearrange, simplify, derive or translate representations. Keep the source equation and record the parent, purpose, working and applicable conditions. A supported semantic check or qualified scientific review establishes validity. Until then, the transformed form is a marked candidate, not a machine-certified equivalent.

The current publisher accepts a transformed MathML equation with a source-linked transformation record and marks it SCIENTIFIC_REVIEW_REQUIRED. Safe MathML parsing still applies. No semantic-equivalence solver or independent reviewer approval is invented. Silent untracked equation changes still fail.

There is no universal three-hint requirement for A/B products. Use an appropriate number of hints, a short guide, a worked explanation, conceptual repair or a rubric/model response. The existing publisher accepts optional hints and optional guidance while still requiring the complete answer summary, explanatory steps and check. Those fields can hold conceptual reasoning; B is not forced into a numerical algorithm. Frozen Core2's existing ladder hints remain unchanged.

An unavailable numerical evaluator must not prohibit research into that topic. The publisher now permits an unverified numerical candidate in a marked review draft. It checks the exported transcription and source custody but records oracle NONE. Supported numeric families still use their existing evaluator and reject wrong results. Transcription verification must never be reported as scientific validation.

## Research findings and promotion

Maintain exploratory notes alongside accepted production. For a finding retain a source locator, the claim/explanation being considered, its uncertainty and intended teaching contribution. Group findings when that makes the work clearer; do not demand a full engineering packet for every web page viewed. Abandoned searches and irrelevant pages need not become permanent learner content.

When adopting a finding, record the affected concept/source/obligation, the contribution and dependency delta. Promote it into a successor accepted baseline through the applicable scientific/source checks. This is a documented production transition, not a request for owner permission at every discovery. Obtain owner direction only for decisions that actually change owner-controlled scope or release authority.

Research notes may discover connections outside the current 1–3-bucket production packet. Such discoveries do not silently expand the accepted syllabus. Preserve them as candidates; include the relevant dependency closure when they are adopted. Complete agent handoffs retain relevant research rationale and rejected alternatives, not just the final paragraph.

## Local effects, not repeated global resets

Starting a search, saving a candidate or experimenting with wording does not invalidate accepted material. A promoted source/model/prerequisite/depth change invalidates only content and evidence that depend on it. A changed shared prerequisite can affect several Cores; an unrelated new bucket need not regenerate them. Cosmetic edits may require fresh artifact/visual checks without requiring the original scientific research to be repeated.

Current implementation boundary: the publication host builds one immutable packet transaction; it does not yet implement a multi-bucket scheduler or transitive authoring-state invalidation. That integration remains part of the existing work. Its temporary packet-level failure must not be interpreted as a policy forbidding research or progress in independent packets.
