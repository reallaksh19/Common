Consume admitted semantics, Core2 question intelligence, exact teaching receipts and the owner practice specification. Preserve the SOURCE_CORE2 corpus ledger while selecting the requested subset. Every question, hint and solution declares all required capabilities. Every generated question has a supported independent subject validation case and an independent prompt-to-case alignment review. An unsupported family is held for a validator extension, not free-written around the gate. Safe recombination may be new; new semantics require teaching/review work first.

Provide a complete answer path: quick check plus full working for closed questions, or an expected-response rubric for open responses, with current-question verification. Hints must preserve the intended learner decision and avoid disclosing the answer.

Competition construction benchmarks guide design only. Their source bytes, locators and inspected design features must be recorded. They never expand curriculum authority. Explicit owner support and benchmark-waiver decisions stay visible.

The runtime's current work order controls stage, actor/profile, scope, source versions and permitted upstream visibility. A new label in the same conversation is not a fresh instance. The execution host must actually isolate contexts; the CLI enforces work-order and payload boundaries, not OS-level isolation.
