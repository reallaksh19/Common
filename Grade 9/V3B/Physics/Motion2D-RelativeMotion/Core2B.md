### Attempt before reveal
Mira and Dev start at the same tree and finish at the same gate. Mira follows a 120 m curved path; Dev a 90 m path. If travel times are equal, decide what must be same/different among distance, displacement, average speed and average velocity.

<details><summary>Concept metadata — open after first model choice</summary>

**Primary concept:** distance versus displacement under changed path · **Linked:** averages · **Conceptual source:** Core1A B02.

</details>

<details><summary>H1</summary>

First compare endpoints, then paths.

</details>

<details><summary>H2</summary>

</details>

<a id="2b-q01-closure"></a>
### Closure
**Acceptable reasoning.** Displacement is the same because endpoints match; distances differ. Equal time means Mira has larger average speed, while average velocities are the same.

**Alternative valid approach.** A diagram with common start/finish and two paths is sufficient.

**Criteria / rubric.** A complete response: same displacement; different distance; correct average-speed comparison; same average velocity.

**Misconception feedback.** Using 120 or 90 m as displacement confuses path with endpoint change.

**Final closure.** Same endpoints fix displacement, not distance.

**Demand-score reason.** Inference 1/2; representation 1/2; constraints 0/2; algebra 0/2; novelty 1/2. Hints alter support, not demand.
