## Prerequisite bridge

**Pythagoras:** a right triangle with perpendicular legs `a,b` has diagonal `√(a²+b²)`. **Two-equation reasoning:** a meeting in a plane must satisfy both coordinate conditions using the same time. These bridges are worked before use; no trigonometry or calculus is assumed.

## B01 — Observer, frame, axes, origin and common clock

Motion is described relative to a named frame. Choose an origin, axes and one shared clock before comparing simultaneous motion. A bag may be at rest relative to a bus while moving relative to the ground.

<a id="b02-path-distance-displacement-and-average-quantities"></a>
## B02 — Path, distance, displacement and average quantities

Distance adds path lengths; displacement is final minus initial position. Average speed uses distance/time; average velocity uses displacement/time. For 6 m east then 8 m north, distance is 14 m and displacement magnitude is 10 m.

![Path, distance, displacement and average quantities](diagrams/path-displacement.svg)

**Deeper help:** [Core 1A B02](Core1A.md#b02-path-distance-displacement-and-average-quantities) · **Reconstruct it:** [Core 1B B02](Core1B.md#b02-path-distance-displacement-and-average-quantities)

**Quick check.** Walk 3 m east then 4 m north. Give distance and displacement magnitude.

<a id="c1-q02-answer"></a>
**Answer.** Distance = 7 m; displacement magnitude = 5 m.

## B03 — Coordinates, components and reconstructing a vector visually

For coordinates use `Δx=x_f−x_i`, `Δy=y_f−y_i`. Components may be negative; magnitude is nonnegative and follows Pythagoras for perpendicular axes.

![Coordinates, components and reconstructing a vector visually](diagrams/component-triangle.svg)

**Deeper help:** [Core 1A B03](Core1A.md#b03-coordinates-components-and-reconstructing-a-vector-visually) · **Reconstruct it:** [Core 1B B03](Core1B.md#b03-coordinates-components-and-reconstructing-a-vector-visually)

## B04 — Constant motion in two coordinates; tables and graphs

For constant velocity use `x=x0+v_x t` and `y=y0+v_y t` with the same `t`. A coordinate table, a path and x(t)/y(t) graphs are different representations of one motion.

<a id="b05-one-dimensional-relative-motion-units-and-signs"></a>
## B05 — One-dimensional relative motion, units and signs

Choose a positive direction and convert units first. Relative velocity is **target minus observer**: `v_A/B=v_A/G−v_B/G`. Equal velocities mean constant separation, not necessarily zero separation.

![One-dimensional relative motion, units and signs](diagrams/relative-1d-signline.svg)

**Deeper help:** [Core 1A B05](Core1A.md#b05-one-dimensional-relative-motion-units-and-signs) · **Reconstruct it:** [Core 1B B05](Core1B.md#b05-one-dimensional-relative-motion-units-and-signs)

**Question record — ID:** `C1-Q05` · **Source:** Agent-authored self-check · **Primary:** 1D relative-velocity order · **Linked:** signs, units · **Bucket:** B05 · **Syllabus:** REQUESTED_ENRICHMENT · **Demand:** entry (3/10) · **Support:** light scaffold · **Answer:** [c1-q05-answer](#c1-q05-answer)

**Quick check.** East is positive. A moves east at 7 m/s and B east at 2 m/s. Find A relative to B.

<a id="c1-q05-answer"></a>
**Answer.** `+5 m/s`, so 5 m/s east relative to B.

## B06 — Relative velocity in two dimensions and observer reversal

The same subtraction works component by component. Reverse the observer and every component changes sign while magnitude stays the same. Relative position uses the same order.

![Relative velocity in two dimensions and observer reversal](diagrams/relative-subtraction.svg)

## B07 — Meeting requires the same position at the same time

A meeting requires `x_A=x_B` **and** `y_A=y_B` at one same admissible time. A geometric path crossing alone does not prove collision.

![Meeting requires the same position at the same time](diagrams/collision-pair.svg)

## B08 — Boat/current and rain/observer model transfer; ambiguity

Name frame chains. For a boat, `v_B/G=v_B/W+v_W/G`; crossing time uses the across-river component. For rain, `v_R/O=v_R/G−v_O/G`. If directions are missing, underdetermination is a valid scientific conclusion.

![Boat/current and rain/observer model transfer; ambiguity](diagrams/boat-current.svg)

**Deeper help:** [Core 1A B08](Core1A.md#b08-boatcurrent-and-rainobserver-model-transfer-ambiguity) · **Reconstruct it:** [Core 1B B08](Core1B.md#b08-boatcurrent-and-rainobserver-model-transfer-ambiguity)
