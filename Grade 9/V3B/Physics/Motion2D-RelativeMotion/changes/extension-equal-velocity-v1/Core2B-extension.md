## EXT-2B-P01 — prediction transfer

Two drones are 30 m apart and have equal constant velocity `(2,−1) m/s` in the same ground frame. Without calculating either final ground position, state what happens to their separation and justify.

**Acceptable reasoning:** their relative velocity is `(2,−1)−(2,−1)=(0,0)`, so the relative position vector is constant. Their separation therefore remains 30 m. A different initial relative direction is allowed; its vector stays unchanged too.

**Alternative route:** over any common time interval the two drones acquire the same displacement, so subtracting their positions cancels that equal displacement.

**Rubric:** identifies zero relative velocity; retains nonzero initial relative position; concludes constant 30 m separation; does not call the drones stationary on ground.

**Misconception feedback:** zero relative velocity means no change in relative position, not zero relative position.
