## Predict before reveal — EXT-C1B-Q01

A begins 12 m east of B. Both move west at `3 m/s`, so with east positive both have velocity `−3 m/s`.

Before calculating, predict: after 5 s will they be 0 m, 12 m or 30 m apart? Then explain whether `v_A/B=0` makes either object stationary on the ground.

<details><summary>Expected reasoning and feedback</summary>

`v_A/B=(−3)−(−3)=0 m/s`. Equal velocity means the relative position is constant, so the separation remains **12 m**. Neither object is ground-stationary; both move west at 3 m/s.

A plausible wrong response is “0 m apart because relative velocity is zero.” Targeted repair: relative **velocity** tells how fast the relative **position changes**. A zero rate of change preserves the starting 12 m; it does not replace it with zero.

**Retry:** Suppose the starting gap were 20 m with the same two velocities. The gap would remain 20 m for every later time.

</details>
