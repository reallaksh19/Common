A starts at `x=−4 m`; B starts at `x=16 m`. Both travel east at `5 m/s`. Find (a) A's velocity relative to B, (b) their separation after 12 s, and (c) whether either is stationary in the ground frame.

**H1:** Compare velocities separately from positions.  
**H2:** `v_A/B=5−5`; keep the initial 20 m gap.  
**H3:** `x_A=−4+5t`, `x_B=16+5t`; subtract at the same t.

<a id="ext-q-001-solution"></a>
### Fully explained solution

(a) `v_A/B=0 m/s`.  
(b) Initial relative position is `−4−16=−20 m`, so the separation magnitude stays 20 m. At 12 s the positions are 56 m and 76 m, confirming 20 m.  
(c) Neither is stationary on the ground: each has ground velocity +5 m/s east.

**Independent check:** both gain the same 60 m displacement in 12 s, so their difference cannot change.
