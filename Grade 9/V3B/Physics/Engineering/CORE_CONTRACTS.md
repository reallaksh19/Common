# Six-Core authoring and evidence contract

Apply the [research and minimum-criteria policy](../Blueprint/V3B-Research-and-Minimum-Criteria.md). Provisional research/drafting is allowed; accepted-source custody, scientific correctness, coverage and self-help closure remain mandatory. A/B support has no universal hint-count quota.

Mandatory realization detail: [V3B production blueprint](../Blueprint/V3B-Production-Blueprint.md). Apply its depth, per-Core action, full-answer, exposure and bound-publication requirements alongside this contract. Structural proof checks do not implement the complete acceptance suite.

Core1 basic notes and Core2 frozen questions/hints remain existing input products. This extension governs detailed Core1A/1B/2A/2B realizations without modifying either.

| Core | Dominant learner job | Mandatory substantive evidence |
|---|---|---|
| 1A | Understand explained concepts and worked reasoning | Setup, meaningful representation, symbols/conditions, non-obvious transitions, worked illustration, check |
| 1B | Reconstruct and generalize concepts | Elicitation before reveal, diagram/relationship reconstruction, targeted help, expected response, misconception repair |
| 2A | Learn expert solution anatomy | Traceable question, cue interpretation, setup, model/representation, complete solution and verification |
| 2B | Recognize/select/transfer | Attempt before model reveal, progressively available support, full solution/rubric, explicit changed/invariant structure |

A can include generative checks and B can include explanatory repair. Page grammar alone cannot establish purpose: a reviewer must inspect the actual learner action and solution path. Declarative does not mean unexplained equations; open-ended does not mean answerless prompts.

## Independent control axes

Core1A/1B: a shared subtopic bucket and intrinsic badge. EASY/LOW uses compact complete reasoning and no enrichment search. MEDIUM/HARD require research contribution records and finer decomposition where needed. Approximate 10/20/30 page allowances are planning capacity, not mandatory lengths. Never remove an inference to hit a page target; split coherent sub-subtopics or record an owner exception.

Core2A/2B: percentage with source/scope/date and a named calibration policy, OR an explicit owner waiver with actual authorization and selected support/question-demand/transfer-demand. Purpose STARTER/PRACTICE/REVISION/COMPETITION stays separate. A low percentage cannot forbid the owner’s competitive destination; it can require prerequisite support and bounded progression. An aggregate score cannot assert subtopic mastery.

A pre-use fit result is a prediction. Owner-waived output is OWNER_ROUTED_NOT_KNOWLEDGE_VALIDATED. No universal percentage bands or mastery's cutoff are claimed here. Specimens use a clearly labeled design preset; absence of a real learner/waiver keeps personalized release blocked.

## Custody and omission controls

Freeze an independently checked source inventory with stable IDs for facts/data, equations, assumptions, symbols, examples, questions/subparts, diagrams and prerequisites. Coverage is computed against that frozen inventory, not the author's list of what it chose to include.

Every applicable asset receives a per-Core disposition: REALIZED, TRANSFORMED, REFERENCE, NOT_APPLICABLE or HELD. Mandatory assets require actual content objects. Transformation records parent ID, changed learner action and why it is useful. NOT_APPLICABLE needs justification and cannot excuse a baseline-mandatory asset. Product audit and rendered-artifact audit are distinct.

Do not equate a field called content with scientific sufficiency. The validator detects missing objects and mismatched contracts; independent review checks every consequential equation, diagram-to-term connection and reasoning transition against the original source.

## All questions, including embedded prompts

Every learner prompt has a question record, its visible source classification and an answer plus explanatory resolution/verification. A task requiring a specific scientific drawing needs a realized model drawing plus acceptance features; see the blueprint for divergent-design rubric cases. A research question with no known unique result needs a model-bound result or evaluation rubric with uncertainty, not an invented canonical discovery.

Frozen source question number/subpart and canonical stem remain intact. The source object binds figure/options/conditions where applicable; the minimal proof checker covers text-only items and may not certify a figure-dependent exam item. A production adapter must verify all original fields and exact source bytes.

Author-created or adapted questions retain new identities and truthful labels; a parent content reference is not an official question source. Source questions with damaged/ambiguous originals stay held. No author-generated preview may be called owner-frozen.

## Reuse and similarity

Fundamental relations may recur with appropriate local meaning and conditions. Reusing a worked example as a reconstruction anchor requires a reason; it cannot count as independent transfer. B products must not expose the requested model in a badge before an attempt that tests model choice.

Compare exact identity, lexical shingles and containment, diagram structure, problem-family/givens/target/solution path, and learner action. Registry policy thresholds are ENGINEERING review triggers. No universal embedding cutoff and no “maximum percent overlap for a whole book” rule.

Initial triage: five-word Jaccard >=0.65 candidate, >=0.80 review, >=0.90 high review; containment >=0.80 review for passages of >=50 words. Exact nonempty matches are reviewed even when short. Equations are classified separately; short technical phrases need human judgment. Low similarity does not prove conceptual novelty.

Before calibrated use: two independent labels, boundary cases, disagreement adjudication, held-out evaluation split by family/topic to prevent leakage, precision/recall/false-block reporting, and versioned approval. All current scores remain uncalibrated. A consequential unresolved scientific or learning defect holds the affected learner-ready acceptance. A similarity score or advisory alone does not; record its triage and rationale.

## Useful badges and hidden metadata

Show bucket, concept, intrinsic difficulty or question demand (label which), Core purpose, support level, source and help location. Show prerequisites and typed linkages where they guide study. Do not clutter a learner page with engineering receipt IDs, test codes or percentage provenance.

Keep internally: original question ID, gate/baseline versions, source digest, research status, model assumptions, transfer dimensions, review state, release state and learner-fit uncertainty. If model recognition is assessed, defer revealing its concept/family badge until the answer section.

## Release sequence

1. Exact source inventory reconciled.
2. Gate baseline and dependency closure checked.
3. Independent Physics and pedagogy review of obligations.
4. Per-Core material, differentiation and answer closure.
5. Learner controls for applicable Cores.
6. Artifact-bound coverage and source checks.
7. Actual visual/layout inspection.
8. Owner release.

The current pure checker is a structural acceptance prerequisite. It always reports release_authorized=false. External reviewer authority and file digest verification need a trusted execution host. The old four-Core runtime is not silently certified by running this checker.
