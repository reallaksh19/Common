# Position-Time Graph Slope QA

Use this checkpoint set when a student-facing batch contains position-time graphs, especially graph-height versus graph-slope interpretation or chord-versus-tangent reasoning.

## AD-77 X-T HEIGHT VS SLOPE SEPARATION

- The vertical coordinate must remain position/displacement as defined by the source.
- Slope must be tied to velocity.
- A high horizontal segment must remain compatible with `v = 0`.
- Negative slope means negative velocity under the chosen axis; it does not automatically mean negative position.
- Teacher-added labels must not imply that a higher x-t graph point means greater speed.

Fail if page composition can plausibly teach graph height as speed.

## AD-78 CHORD VS TANGENT SUPPORT

- Average velocity over an interval must be tied to a chord/secant between endpoint positions.
- Instantaneous velocity must be tied to the tangent slope at one instant.
- The two constructions must be visually distinguishable and correctly labelled.
- On independent-transfer pages, do not add a completed chord/tangent if the source requires the learner to construct it.

Fail if chord and tangent are interchangeable, mislabeled, or too visually ambiguous to distinguish at normal viewing size.

## Required review sequence

1. Verify x and t axis identities.
2. Inspect all slope-sign labels.
3. Confirm high-horizontal examples remain stationary rather than “fast.”
4. Check chord and tangent labels against the source meaning.
5. Render at normal viewing size and verify labels are legible and non-overlapping.
6. Record `xt_height_slope_findings = 0` and `chord_tangent_findings = 0` before release.
