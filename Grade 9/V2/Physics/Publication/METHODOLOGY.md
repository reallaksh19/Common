# Methodology

1. Material learner content is derived from merged LearningDesign blocks, not benchmark pages.
2. Each material item binds one or more exact `source_design_block_id` values plus canonical refs.
3. Probe-first targets are published before any corrective content for those targets.
4. Physics representations must carry semantic payload: system, frame, phase boundary, state continuity, sign/direction, or model transition.
5. Every material item appears exactly once in PublicationStructure and exactly once in renderer-emitted physical placement evidence.
6. The renderer emits placements while producing exact PDF bytes; planned coordinates are not accepted as custody evidence.
7. Independent validation re-hashes the PDF and reconciles semantic items, structure, placements, page bounds, and manifest digests.
8. PDF rendering is deterministic for fixed inputs.
9. `PUBLICATION_ENGINEERING=PASS` never promotes subject, pedagogy, visual, or benchmark validation.
10. Upstream LearningDesign is immutable.
