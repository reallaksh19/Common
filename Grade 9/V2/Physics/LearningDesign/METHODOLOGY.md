# Methodology

1. Consume the merged StudyModel unchanged; design cannot rewrite treatment/readiness or diagnosis.
2. Cover every StudyModel target and every study requirement with at least one authored block.
3. State-handoff repair is a coherent choreography, not a checklist of roles.
4. `MINIMAL_CONTRAST` holds system, frame, phase boundary and physical situation fixed; only the focal handoff choice changes.
5. Worked reasoning exposes state propagation explicitly, not answer-only substitution.
6. Guided → faded → independent support levels must strictly decrease.
7. Independent work carries no conceptual hint or preselected boundary state.
8. Transfer must change physical structure, not only numbers.
9. Verification is embedded in reasoning via continuity, units, sign/direction and plausibility.
10. `PROBE_FIRST` targets begin with the exact canonical probe and no corrective payload before it.
11. Frozen support assets are separate from live interaction support state. Repeated same-route failure selects a pre-approved route-changing conceptual support.
12. Shared arithmetic support remains access/execution support; it cannot redefine Physics truth.
13. Publication coordinates, pagination, renderer fields, scheduling and benchmarks are forbidden.
