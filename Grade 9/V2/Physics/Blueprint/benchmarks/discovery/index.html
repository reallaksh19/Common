Academician Pedagogical Guide: Discovery vs Engineering Authorization

1. Why Candidate Ranking Must Never Grant Technical Authorization

In high-stakes examinations (CBSE, IIT-JEE Main, IIT-JEE Advanced, NSEP Olympiad), physics queries are frequently incomplete: "Energy conservation", "Tension in string", or "Newton laws". If an autonomous engine automatically admitted Rank 1 into manuscript generation, an ambiguous query would silently collapse distinct physical domains (e.g. unconditional mechanical conservation vs general first law thermodynamics). Under Physics Blueprint, candidate discovery is explicitly non-authoritative (CANDIDATE_DISCOVERY_ONLY) and requires confirmed Exact-ID selection.

2. Ambiguity Preservation Across Grades 9 to 11

A query like "Laws of motion" spans Grade 9 foundation (PHY-FORCE-NEWTON-LAWS) and Grade 11 rigorous calculus vector formulations (PHY-NLM-FIRST-LAW, PHY-NLM-SECOND-LAW, PHY-NLM-THIRD-LAW). The benchmark enforces an Ambiguity Preservation Rate ≥ 90%, ensuring that all physically and pedagogically valid candidate interpretations appear in the candidate receipt for human teacher/student discrimination.

3. Controlled Vocabulary as a Cognitive On-Ramp

Secondary school students use colloquialisms and Hindi vernacular terms ("karya aur urja", "gati ke niyam", "upthrust"). The governed vocabulary acts as a bridge from colloquial learner language to formal structural engineering gates without polluting the physical core with informal idioms.
