# Physics Core2B v6 — transfer reconstruction TTUs

Core2B is generative-dominant transfer. It consumes only legal Core2A items/variants and observed Core1B evidence.

## Transfer TTU rule

A Core2B challenge must be a **TTU v2 transfer-reconstruction realization** when the technical representation/model is part of the demand.

The learner should encounter:

`unfamiliar legal question → incomplete / unselected technical state → learner reconstructs or selects → optional fixed help → working → independent verification → canonical reveal → what stayed invariant / what changed`

## Meaningful reconstruction

The missing structure must matter physically: a frame label, force, vector direction, component, event marker, graph feature, equation term, model condition, boundary condition or intermediate state.

Cosmetic blanks and arbitrary hidden numbers do not create transfer.

## Help and answer reveal

Help is preauthored and bound to omitted element IDs. Full canonical completion is revealed only after attempt. Correctness after H3/full solution is learning evidence, not independent-transfer mastery.

## Independent verification

Before reveal, the learner must have at least one structural check available: inverse relation, substitution-back, limiting case, dimensions, sign/direction, conservation, graph behaviour, boundary condition, alternate representation or model-validity check.

## Freshness

Where the legal pool permits, Core2B should use a different legal item/variant from the Core2A worked exemplar so the learner must recognize the invariant rather than replay a memorized state.
