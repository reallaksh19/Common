# Physics Core2B v5 — Generative Transfer through Technical Teaching Units

## Purpose

Core2B is a self-guided generative-dominant transfer product.

> Can the learner recognize the hidden structure, construct/select the right technical representation and transfer the model when the surface changes?

Student knowledge % or owner override controls sequencing/support only. Core2B never expands legality.

## Transfer TTU

A substantive transfer item should use:

```text
UNFAMILIAR QUESTION
→ LEARNER EXTRACTS SETUP / FRAME / EVENT / UNKNOWN
→ LEARNER SELECTS OR CONSTRUCTS TECHNICAL REPRESENTATION
→ LEARNER SELECTS GOVERNING RELATION / FIRST MOVE
→ LEARNER WORKING
→ OPTIONAL PROGRESSIVE HELP
→ FULL TECHNICAL CHECK
→ VERIFY
→ WHAT STAYED INVARIANT / WHAT CHANGED
→ REPAIR
```

If choosing a representation/model is part of the transfer demand, it must happen before solution reveal.

## What does not count

- prose-only challenge when the capability is diagram/equation dependent;
- generic comparison cards standing in for Physics model selection;
- a supplied complete diagram when the transfer demand is to construct/choose that representation;
- a final answer without technical working;
- one-for-one mirroring of Core2A when fresh legal items/variants exist.

## Transfer dimensions

Vary dimensions independently where legal:

- structural distance;
- sign/direction;
- target unknown;
- representation change;
- model discrimination;
- constraint/reverse problem;
- multi-step bridge;
- synthesis;
- competitive mixing;
- surface/context distance.

The learner should be told after the attempt what stayed invariant and what changed.

## Hint ladder

- **H1 NOTICE** — identify the hidden event/frame/model clue;
- **H2 REPRESENT** — cue the representation type without completing it;
- **H3 START** — give the first executable relation/construction.

Full solution exposure is teaching, not independent transfer evidence.

## Model discrimination

When model selection is the task, present Physics evidence the learner must classify: forces, frames, events, graphs, geometry, quantities or constraints.

Generic A/B/C cards with only prose labels are not adequate technical representations.

## Technical checks

The post-attempt check should normally include:

1. setup extraction;
2. representation/model;
3. governing relation;
4. intermediate working;
5. final result;
6. sign/direction/unit/limit/model check;
7. invariant vs changed demand dimension.

## Layout requirements

Figures must be proportional to the technical information they carry, cropped, legible and adjacent to the working they support. Oversized empty axes and tiny labels fail release.

## Authority boundary

- exact Core2A legal-pool ref/digest/item ID remain mandatory in production runtime;
- Core2B cannot create/legalize items;
- owner override cannot expand legality;
- only successful sufficiently low-hint observed transfer may advance robustness.
