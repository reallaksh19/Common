# Physics Core2B — evidence-gated transfer runtime + Generative Transfer Coach

Core2B selects the next learner experience from an exact Core2A legal pool. It has no authority to legalize, rewrite or generate transfer items.

At the learner surface, Core2B is now explicitly a **self-guided generative transfer product**:

> **Can the learner recognize, select and transfer the capability when the surface changes?**

The canonical learner-product grammar is defined in `LEARNER_PRODUCT_SPEC_v2.md`.

## Learner-product rule

The unfamiliar problem comes first. Core2B must not reteach the entire concept before the attempt.

```text
UNFAMILIAR QUESTION
-> first-move attempt
-> solution attempt
-> H1 NOTICE
-> H2 REPRESENT
-> H3 START
-> try again
-> CHECK AFTER ATTEMPT
-> what stayed the same?
-> what changed?
-> smallest repair route
```

Every challenge has a local worked resolution. A full-solution reveal is support, not independent-transfer evidence.

## Two independent gates

1. **Core2A legality:** exact product ref + digest and exact item ID.
2. **Learner evidence:** every capability required by the selected Core2A item must meet the item's evidence floor.

There is no session-wide mastery flag. Capability ceilings are derived from a bound Core1B independent-release receipt plus append-only observed Core2B transfer events.

## Transfer demand

`T0..T8` may be displayed as a convenient ordering label, but authorization is multidimensional:

- structural distance;
- representation change;
- model discrimination;
- multi-step bridge;
- synthesis;
- competitive mixing.

Discrimination and synthesis are independent demands, not consecutive mastery states. Representation transfer requires matching successful observed representation evidence.

## Error and repair

A wrong answer is not a diagnosis. Automatic classification produces an explicit `HYPOTHESIS`; repair routing uses the smallest explanatory prerequisite set.

Repair returns to Core1B for the missing explanatory bridge, then verifies repair on a different legal Core2A item where possible. Repair requests cannot mutate Core1A or Core2A authority.

## Retrieval

Retrieval state is append-only. An attempted representation is added to successful representation evidence only after a correct sufficiently low-hint observed attempt; incorrect or full-solution exposure cannot silently become representation mastery.
