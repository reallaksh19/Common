Core2B selects the next learner experience from an exact Core2A legal pool. It has no authority to legalize, rewrite or generate transfer items outside that pool.

At the learner surface:

> **Can the learner recognize, select and transfer the capability when the surface changes?**

## V8 transfer rule

Every challenge has a stable question ID, visible source class, legal lineage, canonical answer/solution and example fingerprint. Generated siblings may not impersonate frozen source questions.

Core2B must not replay Core2A by default when a legal sibling exists. Allowed relationships are:

- `FADING_ANCHOR` — intentional same-item fading; never independent transfer evidence;
- `STRUCTURAL_SIBLING` — changed legal instance;
- `FAR_TRANSFER_SIBLING` — changed representation/target/constraint/synthesis.

CCU blocks adjacent exact numeric reuse unless explicitly fading-anchored and blocks high structural similarity with equivalent pedagogical usage.

## Learner fit

LAU v2 requires capability-specific knowledge evidence or explicit owner override. If knowledge evidence is unavailable, the route must say `OWNER_ROUTED_NOT_KNOWLEDGE_VALIDATED`; owner routing may not masquerade as validated learner fit.

## Transfer demand and Problem TTU

Transfer remains multidimensional: structural distance, representation change, model discrimination, sign/direction reversal, reversed target, constraint inversion, multi-step bridge, synthesis, competitive mixing and calculation load.

The learner may be required to generate/select/diagnose/derive/verify before solution reveal. Full-solution exposure is learning support, not independent-transfer evidence.

## Evidence and repair

A wrong answer is not a diagnosis. Automatic classification remains a hypothesis. Repair uses the smallest explanatory prerequisite set and returns to a different legal item where possible.

Representation/transfer evidence counts only from successful sufficiently low-hint observed attempts under the existing runtime evidence contract.
