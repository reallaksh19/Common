# Physics Core2B v7 — LAU transfer Problem TTUs

Core2B asks whether the learner can recognize, select and transfer the capability when the surface changes. It is the generative-dominant realization of LAU-routed legal Problem TTUs.

## Authority path

`Core2A exact legal pool + observed Core1B evidence → CDAU → LAU → Problem TTU → Core2B`

Core2B has no transfer-legality authority and may not manufacture fresh items outside the legal Core2A pool or treat a design-pilot item as production transfer evidence.

## Transfer transformation

A Core2B task should require one or more meaningful learner transformations:

`SELECTION | DISCRIMINATION | GENERATION | DIAGNOSIS | DERIVATION_CONNECTION | VERIFICATION | TRANSFER`

The challenge should expose the unfamiliar problem before naming the model where model recognition is part of the demand. The learner may need to construct/select the representation, identify the problem family, choose the governing model, make the first move and finish independently.

## Lineage rule

Every Core2B item must declare one of:

- `FADING_ANCHOR` — same item intentionally faded; useful for learning but **not independent transfer evidence**;
- `STRUCTURAL_SIBLING` — fresh legal sibling with changed instance while preserving family structure;
- `FAR_TRANSFER_SIBLING` — fresh legal sibling with material representation/target/constraint/synthesis change.

Where the pool permits, `STRUCTURAL_SIBLING` or `FAR_TRANSFER_SIBLING` is preferred for transfer evidence. Item-for-item replay of Core2A is not transfer.

## LAU fit

Core2B support is selected from capability-specific learner knowledge evidence or explicit owner override. Support may change hint visibility, partial representation, prerequisite bridge, ordering or transfer distance, but may not rewrite the question, expand legality or convert helped success into mastery.

## Help and reveal

Help is preauthored and bounded. Available tutor functions include `NOTICE`, `REPRESENT`, `EXPLAIN`, `CONNECT`, `START`, `CONTRAST` and `REPAIR`; only those required by the target reasoning failure are used.

Canonical completion is revealed only after attempt. Correctness after full-solution exposure is learning evidence, not independent-transfer evidence.

## Verification

Before reveal, a substantial transfer task should expose at least one answer-independent check when technically possible: inverse relation, substitution-back, limiting case, dimensional/sign check, boundary condition, graph behavior, alternate representation or model-validity check.

## Evidence rule

Transfer evidence is per capability and requires observed successful low-hint performance. Representation mastery requires successful observed representation use. Automatic error classification remains a hypothesis for routing repair, never a diagnosis.
