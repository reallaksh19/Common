# P-D Methodology — Physics Problem Semantics

## Authority order

P-D is downstream of P-A source custody, P-B item safety, and P-C scope resolution. It re-runs P-C rather than copying its conclusions. Source damage, item validity, frame/sign requirements, model conditions, representation dependencies and blocked states therefore remain authoritative upstream facts.

## Family identity versus family semantics

P-C owns the stable family IDs. P-D owns what those IDs mean as reusable Physics problem structures. The registry must be an exact identity closure over P-C; an extra family is as invalid as a missing family.

## Route semantics

The route is a causal/semantic chain: phenomenon → system/frame → representation → model + validity → state/phase handling → relation chosen from knowns/target → mathematical execution → sign/units/boundary checks → physical verification → interpretation. It is intentionally independent of learner support level.

## Graph semantics

Graph operations are explicit typed semantics. `SIGNED_AREA`, `AREA_MAGNITUDE`, `SLOPE`, `HEIGHT`, `CURVATURE`, `ZERO_CROSSING`, `OPTION_DISCRIMINATION` and `UNRESOLVED_DUE_SOURCE` are not interchangeable. The item profile must choose only operations authorized by its family and upstream representation.

## Demand-vector scale

Most demand dimensions use a bounded **0–4 ordinal load**. `reasoning_chain_length` is deliberately count-like rather than ordinal: P-D v1 permits integer values from **0–16** so multi-step routes are not collapsed into the same ceiling merely to satisfy the ordinal dimensions. The same conditional scale is enforced in both the item-semantic-profile and generated demand-vector schemas. The guide badge remains a derived, non-psychometric label; raw dimension values are not mastery estimates.

## Blocked items

A blocked source still receives a structural family/route/demand representation when P-C has enough Physics identity to map it, but P-D may not manufacture the missing graph/source data or certify an answer. Learner-facing demand badges are withheld while the demand vector remains available for system design.

## Badge semantics

Demand badges are guide labels, not calibrated item difficulty. No psychometric inference is made in P-D.
