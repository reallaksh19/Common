# Methodology

1. Canonical capability definitions remain learner-independent.
2. Reasoning contracts expose checkpoint-level semantics, not topic-level scores.
3. Phase-state propagation is separated from formula/model selection.
4. Physical sign and direction meaning are separated from arithmetic sign manipulation.
5. Canonical error signatures are hypotheses/patterns, never learner diagnoses.
6. Ambiguous evidence routes to a low-confound diagnostic probe.
7. Successful earlier checkpoints survive a later invalid checkpoint.
8. Shared arithmetic/algebra execution is referenced as an external dependency, never duplicated as Physics truth.
9. Synthetic acceptance cases contain no real learner identity or raw learner artifact.
10. Deterministic packaging hashes canonical semantic objects only.
11. Benchmark/reference products are absent from producer inputs and packaging.
