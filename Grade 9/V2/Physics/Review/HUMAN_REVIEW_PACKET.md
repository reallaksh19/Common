# Authorized Human Review Packet — Physics V2

Review the **revised** exact candidate identified by the generated `publication_manifest.json` and `review_package.json`.

Independent gates:

- SUBJECT_CORRECTNESS — verify Physics statements, state-continuity conditions, signs/frames, numeric worked trace and problem givens.
- PEDAGOGY_USABILITY — verify the learner can act on the boundary table, worked trace, support fading, transfer and completion tasks without hidden assumptions.
- VISUAL_USABILITY — verify table readability, typography, workspace, density, hierarchy and page composition.

Record evidence through the shared V2 HumanReview intake. `AI_PRE_REVIEW` findings are revision evidence only and do not count as authorized gate decisions.
