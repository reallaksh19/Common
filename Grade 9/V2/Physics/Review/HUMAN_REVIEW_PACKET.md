# Authorized Human Review Packet — Physics V2

Review the **exact two-artifact Physics P-L candidate** identified by the governed human-review binding. Every real submission must use the shared V2 HumanReview intake and bind the complete exact PDF SHA-256 set.

Independent gates:

- **SUBJECT_CORRECTNESS** — verify Physics statements, state-continuity conditions, signs/frames, numeric worked traces and problem givens.
- **PEDAGOGICAL_DESIGN** — verify the learner can act on model boundaries, worked reasoning, support fading, transfer and completion tasks without hidden assumptions.
- **ASSESSMENT_DESIGN** — verify source integrity, item validity, assumption/model handling, diagnostic-use restrictions and exact representation custody in accordance with `AssessmentReview/physics-item-review-policy.md`.
- **VISUAL_USABILITY** — verify table/equation readability, typography, workspace, density, hierarchy and page composition.

The authoritative rubrics and reviewer registries live in `ExactProduct/review-intake/`. The real reviewer registry is intentionally empty until reviewers are explicitly appointed through repository governance.

AI pre-review can provide exact-byte-bound observations and revision evidence. It cannot authorize a reviewer, submit human evidence, set any of the four human states to PASS, or make the product mature.

After all four governed real-review dimensions PASS, and only then, P-L may authorize the final frozen-reference comparative-validation stage. The comparator is never a producer input.
