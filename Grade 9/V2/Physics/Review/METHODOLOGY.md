# Methodology

1. Bind every finding to the exact baseline artifact SHA, page and content refs.
2. Use `review_class=AI_PRE_REVIEW`; never impersonate an authorized human reviewer.
3. A finding is `RESOLVED` only when the revised semantic product contains machine-checkable evidence of the stated revision.
4. R01 requires a real labeled state-table payload.
5. R02 requires concrete state values and explicit boundary handoff in the worked trace.
6. R03 requires complete givens and strict conceptual-support levels `2 -> 1 -> 0`.
7. R04 requires executable structural transfer + fresh completion tasks.
8. R05 remains an observation requiring human visual review.
9. Reseal exact publication custody after revision.
10. Human subject/pedagogy/visual gates remain PENDING until authorized evidence enters the shared human-review intake.
