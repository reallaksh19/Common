# Core1A / Core1B Bucket Depth Policy v1

This file is the learner-publication interpretation of:

`../Blueprint/policy/core1ab-bucket-depth.v1.json`

## Rule

Core1A and Core1B are always built by stable subtopic/SBA bucket.

Their publication depth is controlled by **intrinsic bucket difficulty**, not by student knowledge percentage.

| Badge | Research | Visual/step depth | Soft capacity ceiling |
|---|---|---|---:|
| EASY | no web research required unless uncertainty demands it | compact but explicit | ~10 pages |
| MEDIUM | web research required for representation/pedagogy planning | detailed | ~20 pages |
| HARD | web research required; sub-subtopic decomposition expected | very detailed | ~30 pages |

`LOW` is a legacy alias for `EASY`.

The ceiling is not a target. Never add filler to create a sense of depth. Depth must come from resolving genuine conceptual and inferential structure.

## Research requirement

For MEDIUM/HARD, research should focus on:

- established Physics representations;
- documented misconceptions;
- useful diagram/graph conventions;
- worked-example structures;
- alternate representations that reduce difficult inferential jumps.

Adapt; do not copy.

## Difference between 1A and 1B

Core1A is **declarative** and solution-level.

Core1B is **generative/open-ended** and concept-level, but it covers the same bucket depth.

A Hard Core1B may therefore also become long: it should contain more coherent episodes and visual reasoning, not more conversational chatter.
