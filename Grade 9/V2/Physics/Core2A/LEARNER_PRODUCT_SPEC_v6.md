# Physics Core2A v6 — LAU-routed worked Problem TTUs

Core2A is the declarative-dominant worked-problem layer. It teaches expert problem recognition and solution structure from the **legal Core2A pool**. Its support density is routed by the Learner Adaptation Unit (LAU).

## Authority path

`frozen/validated Core2 source → Core2A legal-pool authority → CDAU → LAU → Problem TTU → Core2A publication`

Core2A may not rewrite frozen source questions, expand legal families from learner need, or treat generated design examples as legal transfer items.

## Required adaptation basis

Exactly one basis is required:

- `KNOWLEDGE_PERCENT` — capability-specific learner estimate with provenance/confidence; or
- `OWNER_OVERRIDE` — explicit owner reference, reason and support band when knowledge is unavailable.

No silent default is allowed. Adaptation changes support and exemplar selection, never Physics truth or legal-pool custody.

## Problem TTU realization

Each representative worked item must expose the canonical expert state:

`recognize problem family → extract givens/unknown/constraints → choose representation → select model → first move → intermediate working → result → independent verification`

The worked solution must make the representation-to-equation mapping explicit. A large figure plus a final equation is not closure.

## Learner-fit routing

LAU combines learner state, task-demand vector and support band. Task demand remains multidimensional, including structural distance, representation change, model discrimination, sign/direction reversal, reversed target, constraint inversion, multi-step bridge, synthesis and competitive mixing.

Low knowledge does not make a hard family legal; high knowledge does not permit skipping source authority. Routing may add prerequisite bridges, partial representations, worked first moves, bounded hints, extra verification cues or a lower-demand legal sibling.

## Cross-Core lineage

Every worked exemplar carries a CDAU example fingerprint. Core2B lineage must be explicitly classified:

- `FADING_ANCHOR` — deliberate reuse for learning; cannot count as independent transfer evidence;
- `STRUCTURAL_SIBLING` — fresh legal sibling from the same family;
- `FAR_TRANSFER_SIBLING` — changed representation/target/constraint/synthesis while preserving legal capability scope.

Core2A should not exhaustively solve every legal item when that would leave no fresh transfer evidence for Core2B.

## Self-help closure

Every substantial worked Problem TTU must include:

- legal authority binding;
- canonical representation/model choice;
- intermediate expert reasoning;
- independent technical check;
- a downstream transfer/reconstruction design where the legal pool permits;
- explicit source boundary when an example is generated or pilot-only.
