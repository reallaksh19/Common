# Physics V2 — Core1B / Core2B Runtime Product Kit

Status: **ACTIVE under the canonical Physics Blueprint in PR #350**.

```text
Core1 -> Core1A -> Core1B
truth    teaching authority   learner runtime

Core2 -> Core2A -> Core2B
source   transfer legality    evidence-gated transfer runtime
```

A-layers authorize. B-layers execute. Observed learner behaviour may update the Evidence/Control loop with provenance, but B-layers may not rewrite Physics truth, Core1A teaching authority, Core2A legality, source identity or historical evidence.

Core1B binds an exact released Core1A authority ref + digest. Readiness configuration says what must be demonstrated; only observed learner/diagnostic/teacher events prove whether it was demonstrated. Fine-grained atoms are evidence-skippable rather than a mandatory ladder.

Core2B binds the exact Core2A legal-pool ref + digest and exact item IDs. Selection is then gated per required capability by observed evidence. Transfer demand is multidimensional; purpose/retrieval may reorder legal work but can never expand legality.

See `Core1B/`, `Core2B/` and `Blueprint/policy/b-layer-runtime-boundary.v1.json`.
