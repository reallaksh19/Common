
## Authority rule

A-layers authorize. B-layers execute. Observed learner behaviour may update the Evidence/Control loop with provenance, but B-layers may not rewrite Physics truth, Core1A teaching authority, Core2A legality, source identity or historical evidence.

Core1B binds an exact released Core1A authority ref + digest before presenting a learner unit. Unit readiness fields are requirements to be demonstrated, never learner evidence. `INDEPENDENT` requires an observed learner/diagnostic/teacher event.

Core2B binds the exact Core2A legal-pool ref + digest and exact Core2A item IDs. Selection is then gated per required capability by observed evidence. Purpose/retrieval may reorder legal work but can never expand legality.

## Runtime invariants

1. Teaching completion authorizes exposure; observed learner evidence authorizes learner-state change.
2. Fine-grained atoms are evidence-skippable, not a mandatory linear script.
3. Applicability is mandatory when upstream model-validity conditions are required; explanation follows the upstream capability contract.
4. Runtime evidence is append-only and digest-bound.
5. Representation transfer requires successful observed representation use.
6. Transfer demand is multidimensional; `T0..T8` is descriptive ordering, not the sole authorization rule.
7. A wrong answer is not a diagnosis; automatic classifications are hypotheses.
8. Core2B repair is a request to Core1B and cannot mutate A-layer authority.
9. Learner-surface copy may not expose internal runtime labels.
