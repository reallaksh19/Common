# Methodology

1. Evidence is append-only and attempt-scoped.
2. Observations bind merged canonical checkpoint IDs.
3. Positive evidence is preserved when a later checkpoint fails.
4. `REPAIR_REQUIRED` requires independent corroboration under an explicit inference policy.
5. Ambiguous evidence requests a canonical probe rather than confirming a misconception.
6. External arithmetic/algebra execution stays outside Physics capability state unless separate Physics evidence supports a Physics failure.
7. Learner state cannot mutate canonical registries.
8. Unsupported psychological causes are forbidden.
9. Only `PUBLIC_SYNTHETIC` learner fixtures are committed.
10. PR #156 and benchmark/reference material are forbidden producer inputs.
11. Fixed evidence + canonical registry + inference policy yields deterministic bytes.
