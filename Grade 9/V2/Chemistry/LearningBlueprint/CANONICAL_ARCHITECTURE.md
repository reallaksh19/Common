## 1. Executive Summary & Core Doctrine

The Chemistry V2 architecture enforces strict end-to-end mathematical and physical determinism, topic-neutral compiler execution, and absolute separation of candidate discovery from technical engineering authorization.

### The Seven Immutable Invariants
1. **Authority Invariant**: Execution order may vary; authority order may never vary. Ground truth and engineering gates strictly govern all downstream representations.
2. **Zero Discovery Authorization**: Natural language search, semantic similarity, or candidate rank NEVER confers engineering authorization.
3. **Dual-Track Non-Conflation**: Core1 (SDU) difficulty is intrinsic (`EASY|MEDIUM|HARD`) and MUST NOT adapt to learner knowledge percentage. Core2 (LAU) adapts practice demand based on calibrated percentage or explicit Owner Waiver.
4. **Chemical State Rigor**: All chemical transformations must preserve atomic mass, net electroneutrality ($\sum q_i = 0$), and explicit physical state symbols $(s, l, g, aq)$.
5. **Zero Topic Hardcoding**: Compiler engines (`engine/*.py`) must contain zero hardcoded topic names, chemical formulas, or branch logic.
6. **Reconstructable TTU Invariant**: B-layer products (Core1B, Core2B) must require active learner chemical derivation with verified completion keys.
7. **Release Gate Governance**: No product may be published without passing the 5-point Product Governance Gate.
