# Mathematics Learning Design methodology

## 1. Preserve the StudyModel exactly
Learning Design may elaborate how an obligation is taught, but may not delete, weaken or reclassify its engagement/readiness.

## 2. Use strengths as entry points
`USE_AS_ENTRY_POINT / READY` is an anchor, not a reteach directive.

## 3. Equality-preservation choreography
Use:
`ENTRY_POINT_ANCHOR → PREDICT_LEGALITY → RECONSTRUCT → MINIMAL_CONTRAST → learner discrimination → WORKED_REASONING → GUIDED_ATTEMPT → FADED_ATTEMPT → INDEPENDENT_ATTEMPT → VERIFY → TRANSFER → COMPLETION_EVIDENCE`.

Worked equality examples must preserve intermediate identities and perform one legal operation per line.

## 4. Minimal contrast
A valid contrast specifies:
- controlled shared structure;
- exactly one focal distinction;
- prediction before resolution;
- the discrimination target.

## 5. Real fading
Guided support > faded support > independent support. Independent work must not expose conceptual hints, chosen operations, partially completed transformations or answer scaffolds.

## 6. Verification is part of solving
Substitution/equivalence/domain checks are integrated into worked, guided and independent reasoning. Verification is not a decorative final checklist.

## 7. Probe first
A `PROBE_FIRST` target begins with the named diagnostic probe. No explanation/reteach precedes it.

## 8. Static/runtime split
Live attempt state may select only pre-approved support rungs. It cannot author new support, mutate the plan, change StudyModel readiness or infer a new diagnosis.
