# MATH-V2-06 Review and revision

This directory records evidence-bound review of the exact Mathematics candidate produced by merged MATH-V2-05.

It separates **AI pre-review** from authorized human quality gates. AI findings may trigger revisions and may be closed by deterministic re-checks, but they cannot set `SUBJECT_CORRECTNESS`, `PEDAGOGY_USABILITY`, or `VISUAL_USABILITY` to `PASS`.

The human-only quality gates remain `PENDING` until matching reviewer evidence is supplied. Benchmark comparison remains `NOT_RUN` and is reserved for MATH-V2-07.
