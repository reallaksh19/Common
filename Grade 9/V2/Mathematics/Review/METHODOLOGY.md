# MATH-V2-06 review methodology

1. Bind every finding to the exact baseline artifact SHA256 and physical/semantic refs.
2. Distinguish reviewer class from review dimension. `AI_PRE_REVIEW` is advisory evidence, never a human PASS authority.
3. Require a disposition for every `CRITICAL` or `MAJOR` finding.
4. Revise frozen learner-facing realization without deleting LearningDesign provenance or MATERIAL content.
5. Re-run exact publication custody and render preflight on the revised candidate.
6. Close an AI finding only when the revised artifact is named and the corresponding falsifier passes.
7. Keep subject, pedagogy and visual quality states `PENDING` until matching human review evidence exists.
8. Keep benchmark/reference artifacts out of revision inputs; comparison happens only in MATH-V2-07.
