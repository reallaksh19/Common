# M-D methodology — problem structure before pedagogy

The method is intentionally upstream of learner conditioning. First M-C answers **what mathematical authority an assessment item binds to**. M-D then answers **what structural problem family it instantiates, why each reasoning transition is licensed, how the result can be independently checked, and what kinds of reasoning demand are present**. Only later phases may decide what a particular learner should study or which support to reveal.

A family is reusable across surface forms. Variable names, numbers, context skin and equation form may vary while declared invariants remain fixed. Transfer boundaries state which changes still instantiate the same mechanism and which changes create a structurally farther task.

Reasoning routes are semantic state-transition objects, not prose solutions. Route steps are generated from the M-C role sequence and family templates. Repeated roles are allowed; occurrence order is preserved. This makes a compound item such as intersection→new-line represent two distinct representation/execution phases without converting the route into an answer transcript.

Verification is first-class and family-owned. An assessment item is not semantically closed until every M-C verification obligation is covered by its family's verification route.

Demand vectors are descriptive planning metadata. Ordinal levels describe local structural load, not probability of success. The derived guide badge is therefore a publication convenience only and must carry its policy/vector basis and `psychometric_claim=false`.
