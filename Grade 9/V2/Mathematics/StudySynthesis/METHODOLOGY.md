# Mathematics Study Synthesis methodology

1. **Start from the requested goal.** Learner state changes treatment, not the target scope.
2. **Preserve strengths.** `READY` modelling/geometry remain strengths. They may be `VERIFY_ONLY` or `USE_AS_ENTRY_POINT`; downstream algebra failure cannot relabel them weak.
3. **Prioritize bottlenecks without re-diagnosing.** `MATH-EQUALITY-PRESERVATION = DEVELOPING` may be `REPAIR_BEFORE` because policy marks it as a high-dependency bottleneck. That is a study priority, not a misconception claim.
4. **Keep local repair local.** Binomial execution and verification are `REPAIR_IN_UNIT` when they are developing but not required to precede every target.
5. **Ambiguity stays probe-first.** Unknown ordered-pair semantics with an unresolved diagnostic case must preserve `MATH-PROBE-ORDERED-PAIR-SLOPE` and become `PROBE_FIRST`.
6. **Verification is independent behavior.** Prompted verification competence does not establish self-initiated checking. The StudyModel therefore retains an independent verification-initiation obligation.
7. **Bind decisions twice.** Learner-conditioned decisions and obligations cite canonical refs and learner-state reason refs, plus the exact policy version.
8. **Fail upstream on missing truth.** Missing canonical requirement/probe bindings produce `CANONICAL_KNOWLEDGE_GAP`; Study Synthesis does not invent Math semantics.
9. **Stop before choreography.** Learning Design owns ordering, examples, contrast realization, hints, fading, practice, feedback mechanics, and workspace behavior.
