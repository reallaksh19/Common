# Methodology

## 1. Evidence is not diagnosis

The fixture records observable checkpoint outcomes and assistance. The engine may form a bounded diagnostic hypothesis, but an ordinary failure remains `SUSPECTED` and does not itself prove a misconception.

## 2. Preserve the causal path

For each attempt, successful upstream checkpoints remain positive evidence even if the final answer is wrong. The engine does not rewrite earlier success after a downstream failure.

## 3. Assistance matters

`GUIDED_SUCCESS` and `VERIFICATION_PROMPTED_SUCCESS` are not treated as independent success.

## 4. Ambiguity requests information

If a checkpoint is explicitly ambiguous and the canonical interface supplies a targeted probe, the diagnostic case records `DIAGNOSTIC_PROBE_REQUIRED`.

## 5. Verification is two-dimensional

Canonical capability evidence answers whether the learner can perform verification under the observed assistance condition. A separate behavior field records whether verification was self-initiated.

## 6. Descriptive output only

The ResearchLearnerView may report state, evidence summaries, hypotheses and probe requirements. It may not contain `RETEACH`, `REPAIR_BEFORE`, `REPAIR_IN_UNIT`, `PROBE_FIRST`, hint ladders, practice counts, page layouts, or sequencing decisions.
