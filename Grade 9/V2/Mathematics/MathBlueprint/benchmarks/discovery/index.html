Academician Pedagogical Guide: Discovery vs Engineering Authorization

1. Why Candidate Ranking Must Never Grant Technical Authorization

In competitive examinations (IIT-JEE Main/Advanced, IOQM, RMO), students frequently query loosely: "Roots", "Sign of quadratic", or "Auxiliary lines". If an engine automatically admitted the top-ranked candidate into the proof generator, ambiguous queries would silently resolve to an arbitrary subtopic. Under MathBlueprint, candidate discovery is explicitly non-authoritative (CANDIDATE_DISCOVERY_ONLY) and requires an explicit, confirmed Exact-ID selection.

2. Ambiguity Preservation for High-Stakes Competitions

A query like "Roots of equations" spans Quadratic Equations, Theory of Equations, and Complex Numbers. The benchmark enforces an Ambiguity Preservation Rate ≥ 90%, ensuring that all mathematically valid candidate interpretations appear in the candidate receipt for human teacher/student discrimination.

3. Controlled Vocabulary as a Cognitive On-Ramp

Secondary school students use colloquialisms ("middle term split", "shridharacharya rule", "cross multiply"). The governed vocabulary acts as a bridge from colloquial learner language to formal structural engineering gates without polluting the mathematical core with informal idioms.
