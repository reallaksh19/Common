# V2-04 Study Synthesis

Status: fresh-lineage V2 producer component. Tracking: #191, parent #175/#174.

Study Synthesis owns **what this learner should actively study for the requested goal**. It does not own canonical truth, diagnosis, teaching choreography, scheduling, publication layout, rendering, or benchmark comparison.

```text
LearningGoalRequest
+ CanonicalTargetScope
+ CanonicalKnowledgeSet
+ ResearchLearnerView
+ StudySynthesisPolicy
→ StudySynthesisResult
  → LearnerStudyModel | explicit blocking gaps
```

## Authority

Permitted engagement modes:

`VERIFY_ONLY | COMPRESS | USE_AS_ENTRY_POINT | ACTIVE_STUDY`

Permitted readiness modes:

`READY | REPAIR_BEFORE | REPAIR_IN_UNIT | PROBE_FIRST`

Material study obligations may cover conceptual bridges, reconstruction, representation meaning, misconception contrasts, worked reasoning, verification, transfer, and completion evidence.

Every learner-conditioned decision binds canonical references, learner-state reason references, and the exact Study Synthesis policy version.

## Hard boundaries

The StudyModel must not contain lesson order, explanation sequence, hint ladders, support fading steps, practice counts, page/layout directives, renderer primitives, runtime hint state, study calendars, review intervals, or benchmark-derived template choices.

Study Synthesis consumes descriptive learner state. It does not inspect raw attempts or independently infer misconceptions.

Missing subject semantics do not get invented here. They return a `CANONICAL_KNOWLEDGE_GAP`.

## Synthetic proof

The fixture deliberately preserves demonstrated modelling/system-setup capability while repairing equality execution, probing unresolved slope semantics, and strengthening verification. A demonstrated prerequisite may be used as an entry point for a downstream repair target without being reclassified as weak.
