# Appendix B — 20 IOQM-Style Mixed Mock Questions

These are **author-created mock questions**, not official past-year IOQM questions. They are designed from the kinds of structures seen in the verified Grade 9 IOQM corpus and from the methods taught in this guide.

Attempt them without chapter labels. Answers appear only after B20.

## B1. Five-digit numbers and a complement

A five-digit positive integer is formed using digits from \(\{0,1,2,3,4,5,6,7\}\), with repetition allowed. At least one digit must be \(3\).

Let \(N\) be the number of such integers. Find the sum of the digits of \(N\).

## B2. Committee with two logical rules

A 4-person committee is chosen from 8 people including \(A,B,C\). The rules are:

- \(A\) and \(B\) cannot both serve;
- \(C\) can serve only if \(A\) serves.

How many committees are possible?

## B3. Lower bounds in a distribution

How many ordered quadruples of integers \((x,y,z,w)\) satisfy

\[
x+y+z+w=30,
\]

with

\[
x\ge2,\quad y\ge3,\quad z\ge1,\quad w\ge4?
\]

Find the last two digits of the answer.

## B4. Bounded multiset selection

A box contains at most 3 identical A-cards, 2 identical B-cards, 2 identical C-cards and 1 D-card. In how many different multisets of 6 cards is the D-card included?

## B5. Two forbidden adjacencies

How many permutations of \(A,B,C,D,E,F\) have neither \(A\) adjacent to \(B\) nor \(C\) adjacent to \(D\)?

## B6. Derangement

Five letters are placed into five addressed envelopes, one letter per envelope. In how many ways can this be done so that no letter goes into its correct envelope?

## B7. Gap method

Six distinct boys and four distinct girls stand in a row so that no two girls are adjacent.

If the number of arrangements is

\[
K(6!)(4!),
\]

find \(K\).

## B8. Alternating relative order

How many permutations \(a_1a_2a_3a_4\) of \(1,2,3,4\) satisfy

\[
a_1<a_2>a_3<a_4?
\]

## B9. Dictionary rank with repeated letters

All distinct arrangements of the letters of BANANA are written in dictionary order using

\[
A<B<N.
\]

What is the rank of BANANA?

## B10. Circular separation

Four distinct boys and three distinct girls sit around a circular table. Rotations are considered the same. No two girls may sit next to each other.

How many seatings are possible?

## B11. Bracelet symmetry

Six beads form a bracelet. Exactly three are red and three are blue. Two bracelets are considered the same if one can be obtained from the other by a rotation or a reflection.

How many different bracelets are possible?

## B12. Pairings with two forbidden pairs

Eight distinct people are split into four unordered pairs. \(A\) may not be paired with \(B\), and \(C\) may not be paired with \(D\).

How many pairings are possible?

## B13. Degree-2 graphs

How many simple labeled graphs on vertices \(1,2,\ldots,7\) have degree \(2\) at every vertex?

Find the last two digits of the answer.

## B14. Coloring a cycle

The vertices of a regular hexagon are colored with 3 colors so that adjacent vertices receive different colors.

How many colorings are possible when the six vertex positions are regarded as distinct?

## B15. A basic state recurrence

How many binary strings of length \(8\) contain no two consecutive \(1\)'s?

## B16. Reverse-state thinking

Starting from \(2\), one move replaces \(x\) by either

\[
x+3
\]

or

\[
2x.
\]

What is the minimum number of moves required to reach \(41\)?

## B17. Divisor product and valuations

Let \(P\) be the product of all positive divisors of \(360\). Find the greatest integer \(k\) such that

\[
60^k\mid P.
\]

## B18. Digit residues

How many 4-digit strings formed from \(\{1,2,3,4\}\), with repetition allowed, have digit sum divisible by \(3\)?

## B19. Pigeonhole

What is the smallest integer \(m\) such that every selection of \(m\) distinct integers from

\[
\{1,2,\ldots,24\}
\]

must contain two consecutive integers?

## B20. Winning positions and a modular invariant

A pile contains 25 stones. Two players alternate. On each turn a player removes 1, 2 or 3 stones. The player who removes the last stone wins.

How many stones should the first player remove on the first move in order to force a win?
