## Coverage statement

All 50 frozen Appendix A questions have exactly one primary journey. C15 is retained because it is a syllabus bridge even though the frozen 50-question corpus does not assign it a primary problem. Corpus frequency therefore audits the guide; it does not define the syllabus.
