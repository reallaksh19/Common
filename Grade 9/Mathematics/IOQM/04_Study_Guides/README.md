# IOQM Grade 9 — Study Guides

Student-facing study guides in this folder combine the existing Grade 9 IOQM curriculum with additional practice/reference material without creating new main-topic ownership.

## Combinatorics Study Guide v1

Start with [`Combinatorics_Study_Guide_v1.md`](Combinatorics_Study_Guide_v1.md).

The guide is divided into nine ordinary mathematical subtopics:

1. counting basics, cases and complements;
2. restricted selections and committees;
3. distributions, binomial counts and multiset selections;
4. linear arrangements, blocks and adjacency;
5. order patterns, inequalities and dictionary rank;
6. circular arrangements and symmetry;
7. graphs, matchings and colourings;
8. recurrences and state encodings;
9. number-theoretic counting and divisibility.
