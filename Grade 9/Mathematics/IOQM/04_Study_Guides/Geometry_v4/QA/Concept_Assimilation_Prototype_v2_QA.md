# Geometry five-concept assimilation prototype v2 - QA

## Revision trigger
The first learner-facing prototype was rejected for two substantive reasons:
1. geometry diagrams were schematic/weak and did not externalize the exact structure used in the reasoning;
2. the five concepts read too independently, with insufficient prerequisite/transfer links.

The v2 revision treats those as pedagogical defects, not cosmetic defects.

## Sample-derived design corrections
The supplied Number Theory sample was used as the benchmark for a connected learner journey: understand -> recognize -> start/represent -> execute -> check -> transfer. The supplied Algebra sample was used as the benchmark for an explicit concept chain, compact theorem/method visuals, and readable FIRST MOVE / WATCH OUT / CHECK treatment. Geometry-specific diagrams were rebuilt rather than copied.

## Visual QA changes
- All core diagrams are vector-drawn and legible at final reading size.
- Equal-length journey: side ticks and angle propagation are visible; worked example shows both 40-degree conversions and the resulting 60-degree angle.
- Cevian journey: one decision tree separates median, angle-bisector, altitude and arbitrary-cevian branches; a same-triangle comparison shows how the theorem changes when the cevian status changes.
- Tangent-circle journey: circles are actually tangent in the constructed figures; centres, radii, baseline, centre distance, vertical difference and the compressed right triangle are all shown.
- Similarity journey: parallel line, correspondence and separate linear/area scale lanes are visible.
- Power journey: chord-chord, secant-secant and tangent-secant branches are shown side-by-side; the outside-secant example explicitly marks point order and whole distance.
- Decorative images: 0. Every diagram carries mathematical structure used by the adjacent text.

## Concept-assimilation/linking QA changes
The five prototype journeys are no longer five theorem islands. The learner graph now makes the main chain visible:
`equal lengths -> equal angles -> AA/cyclic cues -> similarity -> ratio/area transfer`.

Cross-links are explicit:
- equal tangents can restart the equal-length angle engine;
- similarity explains the proportional products compressed by power of a point;
- cevian classification selects which metric/ratio relation is licensed;
- tangent-circle compression converts circle tangency into right-triangle metric structure;
- median status links forward to centroid/equal-area facts;
- altitude status links to right-triangle tools.

Each journey includes a statement of what familiar knowledge it extends and what later journey it feeds.

## Concrete mathematics QA
- FIRST MOVE is numerical/geometric in every journey.
- WATCH OUT uses an actual nearby failure/branch error for every risky journey.
- CHECK executes a numerical/angle/product verification.
- Guided practice uses Notice / Recall / Start without answer-first leakage.
- Transfer practice is included.
- Appendix A pointers are readable and do not determine teaching order.
