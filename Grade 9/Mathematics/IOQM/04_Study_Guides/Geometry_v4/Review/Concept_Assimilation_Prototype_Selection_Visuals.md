# Prototype selection, difficulty, and visual obligations

## Badge separation
Difficulty != priority != learner mastery != source status.
The master decomposition stores these separately. Learner mastery remains `UNASSESSED` until self-report plus an unaided first-move diagnostic is available.

## Five concepts
| Journey | Learner badge | Required role | Structural visual obligation |
|---|---|---|---|
| Equal lengths become equal angles | [CORE D1-D4] [HIGH-YIELD] | foundation | nested isosceles/equilateral angle engine; equal sides visibly marked |
| Classify the cevian before choosing the theorem | [CORE D2-D4] [METHOD-SELECTION] [TRANSFER] | method selection | decision tree + triangle showing midpoint/bisector/altitude/arbitrary split |
| Flatten tangent circles onto one baseline | [CORE D3-D4] [VISUAL] [TRANSFER] | representation | circle centres, radii and baseline forming the right triangle that gives `d=2*sqrt(rs)` |
| Prove similarity, then choose length or area ratio | [CORE D2-D4] [TRANSFER] [HIGH-YIELD] | transfer | similar triangles with scale factor `k`; separate `k` and `k^2` lanes |
| Power of a point: choose the legal product | [CORE D2-D4] [LEGALITY] [TRANSFER] | branch legality | three branch diagrams: chord-chord, secant-secant, tangent-secant |

## Concrete-mathematics standard
Every journey in the prototype must contain:
- a numerical/geometric FIRST MOVE;
- a real nearby failure/counterexample in WATCH OUT where risk exists;
- an executed CHECK (substitution/angle sum/length verification/branch validation);
- at least one guided problem with Notice/Recall/Start hints;
- an independent transfer problem;
- readable Appendix A pointers.

## Calibrated addition
The existing syllabus/research base includes arbitrary cevians/Stewart, but Appendix A has no question whose main method is the legacy Stewart node. A single independent variant is therefore authored to prevent a syllabus-side orphan:
`AB=5, AC=7, BC=6, BD=2, DC=4`. Stewart gives
`7^2*2 + 5^2*4 = 6(AD^2 + 2*4)`, so `198=6(AD^2+8)` and `AD=5`.
This is a calibrated practice item derived from existing repo mathematics, not new web research.

## Learner profile
Part 0 is intentionally not generated in this prototype. Before Part 0, collect:
1. approximate geometry knowledge;
2. time before exam;
3. known weak areas;
then score an unaided recognition/first-move diagnostic before revealing hints.
