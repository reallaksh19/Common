# Geometry concept graph - assimilation-first

## Principle
Learner journeys are organized by recognition, representation, first move, execution and legality. Legacy skill IDs remain traceability metadata, not learner-facing chapter structure.

## Major prerequisite spine
`angle closure -> parallel transfer -> equal-length/equal-angle conversion -> congruence/similarity -> ratio/area transfer`

`triangle feasibility -> right-triangle structure -> classify special cevian -> median/Apollonius | angle-bisector ratio | altitude/right-triangle -> arbitrary cevian/Stewart`

`circle angle/chord structure -> cyclicity -> tangent facts -> power of a point -> Ptolemy / tangent-circle compression / Miquel`

`similarity -> coordinate placement when synthetic constraints are expensive -> vectors for centroid/parallelogram linearity`

`locus definitions -> construction / admissibility checks`

## Teaching journeys (split/merge rule)
1. **Angle engines**: close angle sums; transfer through parallels; convert equal sides to equal angles; regular-polygon turn structure.
2. **Proof structure before calculation**: congruence; parallelogram tests; transformations.
3. **Similarity as a licensed ratio machine**: prove similarity first; distinguish length scale `k` from area scale `k^2`; use area ratios when cheaper.
4. **Triangle legality and metric structure**: triangle inequality/type; right triangles; special right-triangle identities.
5. **Classify the cevian before choosing the theorem**: midpoint/median -> Apollonius; angle bisector -> side ratio; altitude -> right-triangle tools; only then arbitrary cevian -> Stewart. This is deliberately one linked method-selection journey.
6. **Triangle centres and concurrency**: centre definitions/radius bridges; Ceva for concurrency; Menelaus for collinearity with directed ratios.
7. **Circle recognition chain**: chord/arc/inscribed-angle structure -> cyclic quadrilateral -> tangency -> power-product branch selection.
8. **Cyclic length equations**: Ptolemy only after cyclic order is verified.
9. **Tangent-circle compression**: move to centres and one baseline; horizontal separation `2*sqrt(rs)` for external tangent circles on a common line; homothety where useful.
10. **Advanced cyclic transfer**: Miquel via controlled circle construction and directed-angle legality.
11. **Representation switches**: coordinates for perpendicular/numerical constraints; vectors for centroid/repeated parallelograms; trig only when it shortens the route.
12. **Locus and construction**: perpendicular-bisector and angle-bisector loci; translate equal-distance language before calculation.
13. **Optimization/mensuration**: put the variable in the target formula, preserve geometry feasibility/integer filters.

## Five prototype journeys and role coverage
1. **Equal lengths become equal angles** - foundational.
2. **Classify the cevian before choosing the theorem** - method-selection heavy.
3. **Flatten tangent circles onto one baseline** - representation/visual heavy.
4. **Prove similarity, then choose length ratio or area ratio** - transfer heavy.
5. **Power of a point: choose the legal product** - legality/branch heavy.
