# Algebra — Simple 3-Day Navigator

## Page 1 — Exam in 3 days? Start here

Do not read the book from beginning to end.

`QUICK CHECK -> FIND WEAK TOPICS -> FIX IMPORTANT GAPS -> PRACTISE -> MIXED TEST`

- **Day 1 — Recognize:** identify the main Algebra patterns and repair weak high-value topics.
- **Day 2 — Practise:** work important weak areas with progressively less help.
- **Day 3 — Mix:** unlabeled mixed questions and quick revision; no major new core skill.

Night before: review triggers, legality checks, and mistakes; stop at a sensible time and protect normal sleep.

## Page 2 — Quick Check: What would you try first?

Default no-profile probe bank uses T1–T10. Personalized editions may select `T1 ... Tx` according to learner knowledge and available time.

Instruction: spend about 1–2 minutes on each; do not fully solve; mark `[OK] knew the move`, `[?] unsure`, `[X] no idea`.

- **T1** — You know `x+y` and `xy`, and need `x^2+y^2`. What would you name first?
- **T2** — The same product `xyz` appears in three nonlinear equations. What single variable would you set?
- **T3** — A quadratic must not have two distinct real roots. What condition do you write first?
- **T4** — A cubic has exactly two distinct real roots. What must the repeated root satisfy?
- **T5** — `P(m)=P(3)` for an integer-coefficient cubic. What expression should you factor?
- **T6** — The first three terms are in AP and the last three in GP. How would you represent the AP terms?
- **T7** — A recurrence asks for a huge index. What should you search for before calculating many terms?
- **T8** — An equation contains powers with a common exponential base. What substitution makes it polynomial?
- **T9** — `x+y+z` is fixed and the target is a maximum/minimum. What structural idea should you test?
- **T10** — A rational equation has poles in symmetric pairs around one midpoint. What shift should you try?

No H1/Notice or method router is shown before the learner marks the item.

## Page 4 — When you get stuck

- I do not know what method applies -> **Notice**.
- I know the topic but forgot the method -> **Recall**.
- I know the method but cannot begin -> **Start**.
- I started correctly but got stuck halfway -> open the linked Worked Bridge / worked example.
- I reached an answer but it is wrong -> check domain, denominator, branch, equality, integer restrictions, and the exact requested target.

Use less help on each successive nearby problem.
