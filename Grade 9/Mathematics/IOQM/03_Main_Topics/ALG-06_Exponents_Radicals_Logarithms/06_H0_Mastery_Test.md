# Mixed Mastery Test

Solve each problem without method labels or hints. Show enough working to make domain restrictions and candidate checks visible.

1. Solve `16^(x-1)=8^(2x+1)`.

2. Solve `sqrt(3x-2)=x` over the reals.

3. Simplify `sqrt((2-sqrt3)^2)`.

4. Rationalize `3/(sqrt5+1)`.

5. Solve `sqrt(x+5)-sqrt(x+1)=1`.

6. Simplify `sqrt(9+4sqrt5)`.

7. Solve `sqrt(x-sqrt(x+2))=1` over the reals.

8. Solve `sqrt(x)=x-2` and justify why any rejected quadratic root is invalid.

9. Solve `log_3(x-1)=2`.

10. Solve `log_x 27=3` over the reals.

11. For natural numbers `2<=a,b<=100`, determine the number of ordered pairs satisfying

`log_a b+2log_b a=3`.

12. A positive integer `a<50` is not a perfect square. Suppose a non-negative integer `x` satisfies

`sqrt(x-sqrt(x+a))=sqrt(a)`.

Find the largest possible `a`.
