# Practice and Transfer Bank

Try each stage without looking ahead. The prompts become less explicit as you move down the page.

<!-- F0 -->
## Reconnect

1. Simplify `27^(2/3)`.

2. Solve `9^x=27`.

3. Simplify `sqrt(200)`.

4. State the real values of `x` for which `sqrt(5-2x)` is defined.

<!-- F1 -->
## Structure with support

5. Rationalize `1/(sqrt7-sqrt5)`.

6. Solve `sqrt(x+6)=x`. Record the sign condition before squaring and check the final candidate(s).

7. Solve `sqrt((x-4)^2)=3`.

8. Write `sqrt(7+4sqrt3)` in the form `sqrt m+sqrt n` with positive integers `m,n`.

<!-- F2 -->
## Reduced support

9. Solve `sqrt(2x+3)=x+1` over the reals.

10. Solve `sqrt(x+1)-sqrt(x-3)=1`.

11. Simplify `sqrt(13-4sqrt10)`.

12. Solve `sqrt(x-sqrt(x+6))=2` over the reals.

<!-- F3 -->
## Mixed independent

13. Solve `log_2(x-1)=3`.

14. For natural numbers `2<=a,b<=20`, find the number of ordered pairs satisfying

`log_a b+log_b a=5/2`.

15. Express the real solution of `2^x=7` exactly using a logarithm.

16. Solve `log_x 16=4` over the reals.

<!-- F4 -->
## Transfer

17. Historical anchor — `IOQM-2023-Q02`.

For natural numbers `2<=a,b<=2023`, determine the number of ordered pairs satisfying

`log_a b+6log_b a=5`.

18. Historical anchor — `IOQM-2025-Q28`.

Assume `a` is a positive integer that is not a perfect square. Non-negative integers `x,y` satisfy

`sqrt(x-sqrt(x+a))=sqrt(a)-y`.

Find the largest possible `a<100`.

19. A student solves `sqrt(x)=x-2` by squaring and reports both roots of the resulting quadratic. Explain precisely what condition must be checked and determine the valid solution(s).

20. Solve `sqrt(log_2 x)=2` over the reals, including every domain condition used.
