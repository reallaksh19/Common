## Q15 correction lock

The exact 2024 printed page states that the three selected values are **not necessarily distinct**. Therefore the learner/teacher mechanism must allow repeated selections. The extremal triple is

`(n,n,n+38)`,

and the acute condition is

`(n+38)^2 < 2n^2`,

with least integer `n=92`.

Any classifier or derivative resource that silently imposes distinct selections is non-authoritative for this item.

## Figure conclusion

The earlier first-pass flags that treated Q22, Q27 and Q30 as possible figure-dependent items were conservative metadata, not source evidence. Exact 200-dpi inspection shows:

- **all eight GEO-01 historical anchors are printed without a problem figure**;
- there is therefore no historical diagram crop to preserve for these anchors;
- geometric relations must be recovered solely from the exact printed wording/math;
- learner-created schematics may be used for pedagogy only if they preserve the textually stated relations and are not presented as historical source figures.
