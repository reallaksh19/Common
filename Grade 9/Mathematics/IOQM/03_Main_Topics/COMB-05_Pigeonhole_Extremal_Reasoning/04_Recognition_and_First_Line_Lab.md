# COMB-05 - Recognition and First-Line Lab

Attempt each item before reading any later solution. For items 1-8, write only the first useful mathematical line or structural setup.

1. Seventeen integers are chosen. Show that two have the same remainder modulo 16.
2. Thirty-one students choose one of 10 clubs. Show that some club has at least 4 of these students.
3. Nine points lie in a unit square. Give a partition that forces two points to be at distance at most `sqrt(2)/2`.
4. Six integers are chosen from `{1,2,...,10}`. Choose boxes that force two whose sum is 11.
5. A finite set of positive integers has the property that no member divides another. Which kind of extremal element is useful if one tries to enlarge the set under an extra divisibility closure condition? State the choice, not a full proof.
6. A collection of intervals on a line is known to have pairwise-overlap restrictions. The target is a contradiction from the shortest interval. What is the first object you select?
7. A problem asks for the number of 6-digit strings avoiding three forbidden properties. Should your first reflex be pigeonhole or inclusion-exclusion? State the structural reason.
8. A problem asks for the minimum possible value of `x^2+y^2` under a real constraint. Should your first reflex be extremal-choice reasoning or inequality optimization? State the structural reason.

## Full short solves

9. Prove that among any 13 integers there are two whose difference is divisible by 12.
10. Place 10 points in an equilateral triangle of side 1. Prove that two are at distance at most `1/3`.
11. Choose 51 integers from `{1,2,...,100}`. Prove that two chosen integers are consecutive.
12. From any 6 people, prove that there are either 3 mutual acquaintances or 3 mutual strangers, assuming acquaintance is symmetric.
