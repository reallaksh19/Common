# Practice and Transfer Bank

Try each stage without looking ahead. The prompts become less explicit as you move down the page.

<!-- F0 -->
## Reconnect

1. A graph has vertex degrees `2,2,3,3`. How many edges does it have?

2. Can a graph have degree sequence `3,3,3,2`? Give a one-line reason.

3. A path with four labelled vertices is properly coloured with three available colours. How many colourings are possible?

4. A triangle is properly coloured with four available colours. How many colourings are possible?

<!-- F1 -->
## Structure with support

5. A graph is `K4` with exactly one edge removed. Find the number of proper colourings using three colours.

6. How many edges are in `K_(3,3)`? Verify your result using degrees.

7. How many unordered pairs of squares on a `4 x 4` board are connected by a knight move?

8. How many edges are in the complete bipartite graph `K_(3,4)`? Verify by handshaking.

<!-- F2 -->
## Reduced support

9. Find the number of proper colourings of a 5-cycle using three colours.

10. A graph has degrees `1,2,2,3,4`. Determine the number of edges.

11. Eight lines each contain exactly three selected points, and every selected point lies on exactly two of the lines. How many selected points are there?

12. How many unordered knight-move pairs are there on a `5 x 5` board?

<!-- F3 -->
## Mixed independent

13. Prove that a finite graph cannot have exactly one vertex of odd degree.

14. Twelve labelled positions lie on a cycle. Colour them using exactly three named colours so that every three consecutive positions have pairwise different colours. How many valid colourings are possible?

15. Prove that every red/blue colouring of the edges of `K6` contains a monochromatic triangle.

16. Ten clubs each have exactly six student memberships counted with repetition across clubs. Every student belongs to exactly three clubs. How many distinct students are represented by these memberships?

## Transfer

17. Historical anchor — `IOQM-2025-Q08`.

A quadrilateral has all four boundary edges and one diagonal. Its four vertices are coloured using four colours, and endpoints of every listed edge must receive different colours. Determine the number of valid colourings.

18. Historical anchor — `IOQM-2024-Q09`.

Determine the number of unordered pairs of squares on a `5 x 5` board that are connected by a knight move.

19. Historical anchor — `IOQM-2024-Q19`.

The edges of `K5` are coloured red or blue with no monochromatic triangle. Determine the number of valid colourings.

20. Historical anchor — `IOQM-2025-Q29`.

A regular polygon is called colourful if its vertices can be coloured with at most six colours so that any five consecutive vertices have pairwise distinct colours. Determine the largest number of vertices for which the polygon is not colourful.

21. Author-created forbidden-subgraph transfer.

A hexagon has all six boundary edges fixed red. Its nine diagonals may be red or blue. Count the diagonal colourings for which every triangle determined by three vertices has at least one red side.

22. Author-created incidence transfer.

Four segments are drawn inside a triangle, each from a vertex to a point on the opposite side. Suppose two segments start at vertex A and two at vertex B, with all four side-points distinct and no three segments concurrent. How many regions are formed? Justify using the intersection pattern rather than a drawing.
