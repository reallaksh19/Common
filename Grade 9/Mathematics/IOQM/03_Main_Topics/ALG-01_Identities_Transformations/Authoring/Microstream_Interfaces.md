# ALG-01 — Wave-1 Microstream Interfaces

Authoring-only.

## A. Factor / expand choice
- invariant: both are representations of the same expression;
- first move: inspect the target before choosing direction;
- misconception: expansion is always progress.

## B. Substitution
- clue: repeated composite expression;
- first move: name the repeated block and rewrite all occurrences consistently;
- boundary: do not introduce a substitution that hides domain restrictions.

## C. Symmetric identities
- useful forms: `(u+v)^2`, `(u-v)^2`, `u^2+v^2`, `u^3+v^3`;
- first move: reconstruct the requested symmetric form from available sum/product data;
- boundary: root-coefficient Vieta derivation belongs to ALG-03.

## D. Equivalence and reversibility
- reversible: add/subtract same quantity, multiply by nonzero known constant;
- conditionally reversible: multiply by expression, divide by expression, square, clear denominators;
- first move: record excluded values/domain before transforming.

## E. Hidden relation / power reduction
- clue: a low-degree equation satisfied by a variable and a high-power target;
- invariant: the relation is a rewriting rule on admissible solutions;
- first move: replace highest power repeatedly; do not solve for roots unless needed.

## F. PYQ/source audit
- anchors: 2025-Q01,Q21; 2024-Q05,Q11;
- all answers independently verified;
- no correction overlay required.

## Lead integration

The student book must feel like one repeated decision: **what form makes the target smallest?**
