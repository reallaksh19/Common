Status: `FROZEN_FOR_DOWNSTREAM_CONSUMPTION`

## Exported learner-owned rule

`TARGET -> CHEAPEST REPRESENTATION -> FIRST MOVE -> CONDITIONS -> CHECK`

## Downstream assumptions that are now stable

A learner who has passed ALG-01 may be asked to:

1. choose factor vs expand based on the requested information;
2. introduce a substitution when a composite structure genuinely repeats;
3. distinguish an identity from a relation true only on admissible solutions;
4. use an elementary low-degree relation as a rewriting rule;
5. reconstruct symmetric expressions from already-given sum/product data without solving individual variables;
6. distinguish equivalent transformations from implication-only/candidate-generating steps;
7. protect zero-denominator/zero-divisor branches;
8. verify candidates in the original condition after a one-way transformation;
9. stop when the requested information is determined.

## What downstream topics must not infer from this interface

ALG-01 does **not** export:
- Vieta or root-coefficient formulas;
- discriminant/root-count doctrine;
- polynomial remainder/factor theorem or general polynomial reduction;
- AM-GM/Cauchy/equality/attainment canon;
- radical/log principal-value or full domain doctrine.

Those remain owned by their canonical topics.
