# ALG-01 — Practice and Transfer Bank

## F0 Foundation

1. Factor `x^2-49`.
2. Expand `(2x-3)^2`.
3. If `a+b=11` and `ab=24`, find `a^2+b^2`.

## F1 Direct

4. Evaluate `1004^2-996^2` without long multiplication.
5. If `x^2=5x-3`, reduce `x^4` to `Ax+B`.
6. Set a substitution to reduce `(y+1/y)^2-7(y+1/y)+10=0`.

## F2 Standard

7. Given `u+v=8`, `uv=12`, find `u^3+v^3`.
8. If `t^2=3t+1`, find `t^6` in the form `At+B`.
9. Solve `sqrt(2x+3)=x` with complete domain/candidate checks.

## F3 Disguised

10. Let `z+1/z=3`. Find `z^4+1/z^4` without solving for `z`.
11. A number `x` satisfies `x^2-x-1=0`. Show that every positive power of `x` can be written as `A_n x+B_n`; compute `x^8`.
12. If `a+b+c=0`, simplify `a^3+b^3+c^3` structurally.

## F4 Preliminary-style

13. Suppose `r+s=5` and `r^2+s^2=13`. Find `r^4+s^4` without solving `r,s`.
14. A real `x` satisfies `x+1/x=4`. Find `x^5+1/x^5` by building a recurrence or repeated identities.
15. Determine all real solutions of `sqrt(x+6)=x` and explicitly identify the step that can introduce an extraneous candidate.

## Transfer

16. Explain why relation reduction in ALG-01 is the conceptual precursor to polynomial reduction in ALG-03.
17. Give an example where expanding destroys a useful factor structure.
18. Give an example where factoring hides the coefficient information you actually need.
