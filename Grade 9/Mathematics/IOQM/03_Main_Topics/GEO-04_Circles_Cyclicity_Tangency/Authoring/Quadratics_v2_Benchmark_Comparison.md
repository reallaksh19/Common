## Geometry-specific benchmark upgrade

GEO-04 adds one requirement that the algebraic Quadratics benchmark did not need: **theorem-legality custody**. Learners must distinguish a circle relation that is visually suggested from one that is actually stated or proved. The student journey therefore makes these boundaries explicit:

- cyclicity must be earned before cyclic relations are used;
- tangency must be stated/proved before tangent theorems are used;
- a power product must be owned by one identified point with respect to one fixed circle;
- coordinates are permitted only after the circle structure to be preserved is identified.
