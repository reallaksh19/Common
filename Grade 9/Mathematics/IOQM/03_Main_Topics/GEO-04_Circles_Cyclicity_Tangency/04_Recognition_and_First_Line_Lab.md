# Recognition and First-Line Lab

For each item, write only the **best first move** or choose the best option. Do not complete a long solution unless needed to justify the choice.

## Recognition

1. Four points `A,B,C,D` form a quadrilateral and the picture looks almost circular. Which statement is safe immediately?
   A. opposite angles are supplementary  B. equal chords subtend equal angles  C. the quadrilateral angle sum is `360°`  D. `ABCD` is cyclic

2. `A,B,C,D` lie on one circle and `∠ACB` and `∠ADB` both subtend chord `AB`. What relation should you test first?
   A. the angles are equal  B. their sum is `180°`  C. both are `90°`  D. no relation

3. `AB` is a diameter and `C` lies on the circle. Which local fact is immediately available?
   A. `AC=BC`  B. `∠ACB=90°`  C. `∠ABC=45°`  D. `AB=AC`

4. A line is stated to be tangent at `T` to a circle with centre `O`. What should be marked first?
   A. `OT` equals the tangent length  B. `OT` is perpendicular to the tangent  C. `T` is a midpoint  D. the tangent is a chord

5. From an external point `P`, two tangents touch the same circle at `A,B`. Which relation is cheapest?
   A. `PA=PB`  B. `OA=PA`  C. `AB` is a diameter  D. `OP=OA`

6. A tangent at `A` and chord `AB` form an angle. Which theorem should be tested before a generic angle chase?
   A. Pythagoras  B. alternate segment  C. quadrilateral sum  D. inclusion-exclusion

7. Chords `AB` and `CD` intersect at interior point `P`. Which product has the correct structure?
   A. `PA·PC=PB·PD`  B. `PA·PB=PC·PD`  C. `AB·CD=PA·PD`  D. `PA²=PC·PD`

8. Two circles intersect at `A,B`. What line relation should you inspect if their centres are known?
   A. line of centres is parallel to `AB`  B. line of centres is perpendicular to `AB`  C. all four points are collinear  D. centres lie on the circles

## First lines

9. In cyclic quadrilateral `ABCD`, `∠A=112°`. Write the first line that determines `∠C`.

10. A quadrilateral has `∠A+∠C=180°`. Write the first structural conclusion.

11. A tangent at `T` to a circle with centre `O` meets a chord through `T`. Write the first angle relation you would mark before using alternate segment.

12. External point `P` has tangent `PT` and secant meeting the circle at `A` then `B`. Write the first power equation.

13. Two secants from external point `P` meet the circle in order `P-A-B` and `P-C-D`. Write the correct power equation.

14. A right triangle, a square and a circle condition all appear with numerical side lengths. Write one sentence explaining when coordinates should be tested.

15. A proposed solution says “`ABCD` is cyclic because it looks cyclic.” Write the missing proof target, not a full proof.

16. Two intersecting circles share chord `AB`; another line through point `A` is also known perpendicular to `AB`. State the structural comparison you should make first.
