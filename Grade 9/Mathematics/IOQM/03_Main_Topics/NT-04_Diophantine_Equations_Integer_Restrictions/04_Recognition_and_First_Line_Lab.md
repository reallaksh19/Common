# Recognition and First-Line Lab

For each prompt, write only the first useful mathematical line. Do not finish the problem yet.

1. Positive integers satisfy `(x-4)(y+1)=30`.
2. Integers satisfy `x^2-y^2=72`.
3. `xy=2^5*3^2*7` and `gcd(x,y)=1`.
4. Integer rectangle area 120; perimeter is to be minimized.
5. Reduced `a/b` is closest to `7/9` under a denominator cap.
6. Integers `x,y` have sum 15 and product 44; target is `x^2+y^2`.
7. A quadratic with integer coefficients is required to have an integer root.
8. Three positive integers satisfy one equation and `a+b+c=40`.
9. You have listed six factor cases. What sentence proves there cannot be a seventh kind?
10. Squaring an equation produced three integer candidates. What must happen before any is accepted?
