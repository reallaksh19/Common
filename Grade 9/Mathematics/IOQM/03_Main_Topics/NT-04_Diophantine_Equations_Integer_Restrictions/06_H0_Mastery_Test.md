# Mastery Check

No method names or hints are supplied on the first attempt. Show enough work to justify completeness, not only a final number.

1. Positive integers x,y satisfy xy=180 and gcd(x,y)=1. Minimize x+y.
2. Find the number of integer pairs (x,y) satisfying (x-3)(y+2)=24 and x+y>0.
3. A reduced a/b with 1<=b<=20 is closest to 5/7 but not equal. Find a+b.
4. Find all integers n with n^2-10n+13 a square and 0<=n<=30; how many?
5. Integers x,y have x+y=17 and xy=60. Find |x-y|.
6. Positive integers x<=y satisfy 1/x+1/y=1/3. Find x+y for the solution with minimum y.
7. Find the least perimeter of an integer rectangle of area 210.
8. How many positive integer triples a<=b<=c satisfy abc=72?
9. For integers x, how many values with |x|<=20 make x^2+x+1 divisible by 3?
10. Find the greatest |m-n| over integer solutions of m^2=4n^2-5n+16.
11. How many reduced fractions a/b with 1<=b<=12 satisfy |a/b-1/2|=1/12?
12. Integers x,y satisfy x+y=20 and x^2+y^2=208. Find xy.
13. Positive integers x,y satisfy x^2-y^2=105. Find the smallest x.
14. Find the number of integer n in [-50,50] for which n(n+1) is divisible by 6.
15. If integer roots of t^2-St+P are distinct and S=12,P=35, find their product difference |r1-r2|.
16. How many positive integers n<100 have exactly one multiplicative partition into factors >1 after order is ignored and including n itself?
