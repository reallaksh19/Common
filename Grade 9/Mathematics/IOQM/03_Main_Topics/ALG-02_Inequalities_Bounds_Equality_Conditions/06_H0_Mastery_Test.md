# ALG-02 - H0 Mixed Mastery Test

No method labels or default hints.

1. Find the real minimum of `x^2-12x+40`.
2. Find the real maximum of `17-(x-5)^2`.
3. For `x>0`, find the minimum of `x+36/x`.
4. Positive `a,b` satisfy `a+b=16`. Find the maximum of `ab`.
5. Positive `x,y` satisfy `x+y=14`. Find the minimum of `1/x+1/y`.
6. Decide whether `0` is a minimum of `x^2` on `x!=0`.
7. Find the integer minimum of `(n-3/2)^2+1`.
8. For integer `n`, find the minimum of `n^2-9n+23`.
9. A proof gives `F>=10`; equality requires `x=y`, but the original condition says `x!=y`. State the strongest justified extremum conclusion without additional information.
10. For nonnegative `p,q`, `p+q=18`. Find the maximum of `pq` and verify attainment.
11. For positive `p,q`, `p+q=18`. Does `pq` have a minimum? Justify.
12. The request is “for which `k` does `x^2+kx+9=0` have a repeated real root?” Route it to the correct canonical topic and state why ALG-02 should not duplicate the method.
13. A continuous relaxation has minimum `5` at `x=2.4`, but `x` must be integral. Describe the exact next calculation required before claiming the integer minimum.
14. A rectangle with perimeter `28` has sides `x,y>0`. Find the maximum area.
15. **WHY NOT:** a student writes AM-GM `a+b>=2sqrt(ab)` to maximize `a+b` when only `ab=9` is fixed. Diagnose why this does not establish a maximum.
16. **Transfer:** construct a function/domain pair where a finite supremum exists but no maximum exists.
