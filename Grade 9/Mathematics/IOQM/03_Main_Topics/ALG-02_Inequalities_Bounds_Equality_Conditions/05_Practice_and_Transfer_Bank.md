# ALG-02 - Practice and Transfer Bank

## F0 - foundation

1. Write `x^2-6x+13` in completed-square form and state its real minimum.
2. State the real maximum of `9-(x-4)^2`.
3. For `x>0`, use AM-GM to bound `x+4/x` below.
4. For nonnegative `a,b` with `a+b=10`, bound `ab` above.
5. Explain why `x^2>=0` does not by itself prove a minimum of `0` on `x>0`.

## F1 - direct

6. Minimize `x+9/x` for `x>0`.
7. Maximize `ab` for `a,b>=0`, `a+b=12`.
8. Minimize `1/x+1/y` for `x,y>0`, `x+y=10`.
9. Find the real minimum of `x^2+10x+29`.
10. Find the integer minimum of `(n-1/2)^2+2`.

## F2 - standard

11. For positive `x,y` with `xy=16`, minimize `x+y`.
12. For positive `x,y` with `x+y=20`, maximize `xy`.
13. Determine whether `x^2` on the domain `x!=0` has a minimum.
14. Determine whether `1-1/x` on `x>0` has a maximum.
15. For integer `n`, minimize `n^2-5n+9`.

## F3 - disguised

16. A rectangle has perimeter `40`. Find its maximum possible area and state the equality condition.
17. Positive numbers `p,q` satisfy `pq=36`. Find the minimum of `2p+8q` after choosing a useful representation/substitution.
18. If `x,y>0` and `x+y=1`, minimize `1/x+1/y`.
19. A real `t` satisfies `t+1/t=5`, `t>0`. Give a lower bound on `t^2+1/t^2` and then determine its exact value from the relation; distinguish the two statements.
20. For integers `m,n>=0` with `m+n=15`, maximize `mn`.

## F4 - preliminary-style

21. Real `x,y` satisfy `x^2+y^2=x+y+1012`. What is the largest possible integer value of `x+y`? (Mechanism analogue of the 2025 anchor; do not use the historical stem as an authored item.)
22. For positive `a,b,c` with `abc=1`, prove `a+b+c>=3` and state all equality conditions.
23. For positive `x,y` with `x+y=8`, minimize `x^2/y + y^2/x` by a justified inequality route.
24. For integer `n`, find the minimum of `n^2-7n+15` and compare it with the real minimum.
25. Determine whether the set `{x+1/x : x>1}` has a minimum; justify attainment rather than quoting a bound alone.

## Transfer T2-T4

26. **T2 representation:** solve the same real minimum `x^2-6x+13` by square completion, then explain why a discriminant route would answer a different natural question.
27. **T3 domain:** change the domain of `x^2` from real `x` to `x>0` to nonzero integers. State how the extremum conclusion changes each time.
28. **T3 number theory:** an inequality reduces an integer variable to `10<=n<=14`. Explain what inequality has accomplished and what remains for the integer owner.
29. **T3 geometry:** fixed perimeter gives a fixed sum of side lengths. Explain why the area maximum is an ALG-02 mechanism inside a geometry surface.
30. **T4 mixed:** create an example where a true lower bound is not a minimum because equality is excluded.
