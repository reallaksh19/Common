# GEO-03 Concept and Dependency Map

## Topic promise
Convert a crowded ratio picture into a short proof chain: **prove the shape relation -> transfer the right kind of ratio -> use area shares before metric lengths -> choose coordinates only when they are cheaper.**

## Knowledge dependency map
`school angle facts + fractions/ratios + triangle area` -> `prove similarity / identify shared height or base` -> `transfer side ratios` -> `square only for area` -> `centroid and area decomposition` -> `changed-surface transfer`.

Borrowed material is retrieved, not retaught: basic angle facts from the Grade-9 core; systematic coordinate/vector technique from GEO-05 when needed; special cevian metric canon routes to GEO-01.

## Method-selection map
| Visible clue | Structural question | First move | Boundary check |
|---|---|---|---|
| two matching angle pairs | are the triangles similar? | write the vertex correspondence | similarity, not congruence unless scale is 1 |
| a segment is parallel to a triangle side | which small and large triangles are similar? | write one side-ratio chain | parallelism must be given or proved |
| corresponding side scale is known | is the target length or area? | use `k` for length, `k^2` for area | do not square a length target |
| same base or same altitude | can area replace length work? | compare the other one-dimensional quantity | no similarity is needed |
| medians meet at one point | is only a ratio/area asked? | use the centroid `2:1` or one-third area share | use coordinates only if they shorten the work |
| intersecting lines inside a simple polygon | are all data ratios? | decompose into ratio-controlled triangles | avoid metric formulas without need |

## Transfer map
- rotate or shear the figure while preserving collinearity, parallelism and division ratios: the ratio/area reasoning survives;
- replace a triangle picture by a square/rectangle line-intersection picture: the same ratio transfer can survive;
- move from synthetic ratios to coordinates as a check: the invariant answer should agree;
- change the requested quantity from a side to an area: remember the exponent changes from `k` to `k^2` only after similarity is established.

## Mandatory contrasts
1. **Similarity vs congruence:** same shape may have different size; congruence means scale factor 1.
2. **Side ratio vs area ratio:** `k` versus `k^2` for similar figures.
3. **Centroid formula vs area reasoning:** coordinates locate the point; median/area structure often answers the question faster.

## Downstream boundary
GEO-03 provides similarity, ratio transfer, area ratios and centroid area structure. GEO-01 owns special-cevian metric relations and triangle feasibility; GEO-05 owns systematic coordinate/vector representation; GEO-04 owns circle theorems.
