# Independent Mastery Check

No method is named. Choose your representation and justify each answer.

1. Find the smallest positive integer with exactly 24 positive divisors.
2. How many positive divisors of `2^9*3^6*5^2` are perfect squares?
3. Find the least positive multiplier that turns `2^7*3^2*5^5` into a perfect cube.
4. Find `v_5(100!)`.
5. How many positive integers at most 1000 have an odd number of positive divisors?
6. Find the greatest `k>1` such that `2^30*3^18*7^12` is a perfect `k`-th power.
7. Let `N=2^6*3^4*5^2`. How many ordered coprime factor pairs `(x,y)` satisfy `xy=N`?
8. Find the smallest squarefree positive integer with exactly 32 positive divisors.
9. A divisor of `2^6*3^5*5^4*7^3*11^2` is squarefree and uses exactly three primes. How many such divisors are possible?
10. Let `N=2^5*3^3`. How many divisors of `N^2` are less than `N` but do not divide `N`?
11. Prove or refute: a positive integer has an even number of positive divisors exactly when it is not a square.
12. Positive integer squares `A^2>B^2` differ by 21. Determine all possible pairs `(A,B)`.
