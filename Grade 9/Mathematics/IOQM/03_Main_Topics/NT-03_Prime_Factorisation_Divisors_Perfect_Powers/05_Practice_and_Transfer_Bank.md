# Practice and Transfer Bank

## Practice

1. Factor `7560` into primes and write its exponent vector.
2. Find the number of positive divisors of `3600`.
3. Find the number of square divisors of `2^7*3^4*5^3`.
4. Find the least positive integer by which `756` must be multiplied to make a perfect square.
5. Find the least positive integer by which `5400` must be divided to leave a perfect square.
6. Find the greatest `k>1` for which `2^6*3^3*5^9` is a perfect `k`-th power.
7. Find the squarefree kernel of `7560`, meaning the product of primes whose exponents in `7560` are odd.
8. Without listing divisors, decide whether `1764` has an odd number of positive divisors.
9. Find the number of unordered positive factor pairs of `360`.
10. A squarefree integer has five distinct prime factors. How many positive divisors does it have?
11. Compute `v_2(25!)`.
12. Compute `v_3(50!)`.
13. Find the greatest `k` for which `2^18*3^12*5^6` is a perfect `k`-th power.
14. How many square divisors does `2^8*3^5*7^2` have?
15. Find the smallest positive integer with exactly 12 positive divisors.
16. Find the smallest positive integer with exactly 15 positive divisors.
17. Find the smallest positive integer with exactly 45 positive divisors.
18. How many cube divisors does `2^10*3^6` have?
19. How many ordered pairs `(x,y)` of positive coprime integers satisfy `xy=2^6*3^4`?
20. How many squarefree positive integers divide `2^4*3^2*5^7*7`?
21. How many integers `n<=500` have an odd number of positive divisors?
22. Let `N=2^4*3^2`. How many positive divisors of `N^2` are less than `N` but do not divide `N`?
23. Find the least multiplier that makes `2^5*3^8*5` a perfect sixth power.
24. A divisor of `2^5*3^4*5^3*7` is squarefree and has exactly three prime factors. How many such divisors are there?

## Transfer

25. A rectangular box has integer side lengths with product `2^6*3^3`. If the three side lengths are pairwise coprime, how many ordered triples of side lengths are possible?
26. A locker code is the exponent vector `(a,b,c)` of a divisor of `2^8*3^5*5^4`. How many codes correspond to square divisors?
27. The integer `m` is known to divide `30!` and has prime factorisation `2^a3^b5^c`. State the sharp independent bounds on `a,b,c`.
28. Explain why "the number of divisors is odd" is a better recognition clue than "list the divisors" for detecting a square.
29. Two positive squares differ by 45. Give all possible ordered pairs `(larger square root, smaller square root)`.
30. A positive integer has exponent vector `(8,4,2)`. Which of square, cube, fourth power, sixth power must it be?
