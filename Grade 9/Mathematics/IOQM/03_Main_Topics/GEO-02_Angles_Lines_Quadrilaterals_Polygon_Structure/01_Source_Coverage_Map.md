## Independent mathematical checkpoints

### IOQM-2025-Q13
Coordinate/vector direction reconstruction from the stated side lengths and 30/90 degree directions gives the requested diagonal angle as 60 degrees. Coordinate work is an alternate representation; this package does not teach general vector canon.

### IOQM-2024-Q04

### IOQM-2023-Q06
For a regular k-gon, interior angle `n = 180 - 360/k`. Requiring the requested integer/even condition reduces to a divisor classification and yields 16 admissible values.

### IOQM-2023-Q24
For bases differing by d and legs p,q, a nondegenerate trapezium exists exactly when `|p-q| < d < p+q`; equality is degenerate. Count unordered base/leg assignments under congruence, not drawings. Result 31.

### IOQM-2023-Q25
Use regular-polygon rotational symmetry and half-turn gap structure to count perpendicular diagonal pairs. The first qualifying side count is 28. Circle-angle canon is not taught here.
