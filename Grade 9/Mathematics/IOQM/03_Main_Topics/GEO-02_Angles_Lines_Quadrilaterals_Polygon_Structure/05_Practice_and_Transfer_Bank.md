# Practice and Transfer Bank

Attempt each problem before consulting the teacher key. The stages deliberately fade support: first reconnect familiar facts, then select structures with less prompting, then finish with independent transfer.

<!-- F0 -->
## Reconnect
State the first relation you are using before calculating.

<!-- ITEM:GEO02-P01 -->
1. Two adjacent angles on a line are in the ratio 2:3. Find them.

<!-- ITEM:GEO02-P02 -->
2. A transversal cuts parallel lines. One acute angle is 47 degrees. List the two possible angle measures in the diagram.

<!-- ITEM:GEO02-P03 -->
3. A quadrilateral has angles 91,88,104 degrees and x. Find x.

<!-- F1 -->
## Structure with support
Use one structural fact to reduce the problem before doing arithmetic.

<!-- ITEM:GEO02-P04 -->
4. A diagonal splits a quadrilateral into triangles whose angle sums leave unknowns x and y. One triangle has known angles 40,65; the other 52,71. Find x+y.

<!-- ITEM:GEO02-P05 -->
5. Find the exterior angle of a regular 24-gon.

<!-- ITEM:GEO02-P06 -->
6. A regular polygon has interior angle 156 degrees. Find the number of sides.
