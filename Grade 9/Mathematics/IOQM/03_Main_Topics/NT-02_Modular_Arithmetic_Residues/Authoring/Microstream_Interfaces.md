## B Legal operations
Addition/subtraction/multiplication/powers preserve congruence. Reduction can occur before arithmetic.

## C Inverses/cancellation
A factor has an inverse modulo m iff gcd(factor,m)=1. Illegal cancellation counterexamples mandatory. Do not reteach Euclidean algorithm.

## D Power cycles / last digits
Choose target modulus, list minimal cycle, reduce exponent only under justified periodicity.

## E Simultaneous congruences
At Grade-9 depth parametrize one class, test the other, check gcd compatibility for shared-factor moduli.
