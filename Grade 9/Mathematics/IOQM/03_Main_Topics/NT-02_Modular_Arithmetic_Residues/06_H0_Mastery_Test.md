# NT-02 - H0 Mastery Test

No default hints or method labels.

## Part A - Notice / first line
1. `83 congruent 11 (mod 12)`: write the divisibility statement underneath it.
2. Find the first line for `7^123 mod 10`.
3. `4x congruent 5 (mod 9)`: write the legality check before solving.
4. `x congruent 2 (mod 6)`, `x congruent 5 (mod 9)`: write the compatibility check.

## Part B - Full mixed solve
5. Find `2^2026 mod 7`.
6. Find the last two digits of `5^2026`.
7. Solve `5x congruent 7 (mod 12)`.
8. Solve `6x congruent 12 (mod 18)` as classes modulo 18.
9. Find the least positive x with `x congruent 1 (mod 4)`, `x congruent 4 (mod 5)`.
10. Find `3^1234 mod 7`.
11. Determine whether `x congruent 2 (mod 4)`, `x congruent 3 (mod 6)` has a solution.
12. Find the last digit of `7^7^7`.

## Part C - Same surface, different decision
13. Compare `14|42` with `42 congruent 0 (mod 14)` and explain the relationship without calling the statements identical in form.
14. Compare cancellation in `3x congruent 6 (mod 7)` and `3x congruent 6 (mod 9)`; solve both.

## Part D - Transfer / WHY-NOT
15. Find the smallest m>=14 for which the residues of `1^4,...,14^4` are distinct.
16. A learner expands `2^100` to find its residue mod 7. Give the shorter structural route and explain why it is valid.
