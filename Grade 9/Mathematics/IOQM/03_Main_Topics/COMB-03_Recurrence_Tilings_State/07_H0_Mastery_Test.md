# Mixed Mastery Test

1. A `1 x 9` strip is tiled by pieces of lengths 1 and 2. How many tilings are possible? Give a structural justification.

2. How many binary strings of length 8 contain no consecutive 1s?

3. How many compositions of 9 using parts 1 and 2 contain no consecutive 2s? Your state must make future legality unambiguous.

4. A `2 x n` board is tiled by dominoes and `2 x 2` square tiles. How many tilings are there for `n=6`?

5. How many length-7 binary strings contain no consecutive 1s and have an even number of 1s?

6. Starting from 1, a move replaces `x` by `x+1` or `2x`. What is the minimum number of moves needed to reach 63? Prove minimality.

7. How many partitions of 10 into distinct positive parts are there?

8. In how many ways can 18 be written as a sum of powers of 2 if each power may be used at most twice?

9. You are counting binary strings with no consecutive 1s and an even number of 1s. A proposed state records only the current position. Explain precisely why it is insufficient and give a sufficient small state.

10. A process allows several legal moves from a state and asks whether a target is reachable. State the additional feature that would turn this into an adversarial game problem rather than deterministic state evolution, and explain why branching alone is not enough.
