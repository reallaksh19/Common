## Purpose

This folder preserves the independently checked problem extraction prepared for the ALLEN Algebra marathon video. The associated ALLEN Algebra worksheet contains 50 questions, so the recoverable worksheet sequence ends at Q50.

## Confidence boundary

The mathematical content in the numbered lots has been independently solved/verified and, where possible, matched to original contest sources. However, an accessible transcript/video anchor establishing the exact lecture-to-worksheet numbering was not recovered.

Therefore:

- mathematical verification and lecture-order confidence are kept separate;
- worksheet numbering is retained for navigation;
- lecture placement remains **Level D — mathematical problem identified only** unless a future video/transcript anchor upgrades it;
- no Q51+ problems are invented or imported from unrelated modules.
