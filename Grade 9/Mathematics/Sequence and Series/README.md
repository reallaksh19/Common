## Learning objective

Build Grade 9 / competitive-foundation structural mastery sufficient for the learner to explain and independently attack the source and bridge questions.

## Concept Book method

`SEE -> REALIZE -> UNDERSTAND -> ADOPT`

- **SEE** — pattern, table, difference/ratio marks, expansion, diagram, or transformation first.
- **REALIZE** — identify the invariant/hidden structure.
- **UNDERSTAND** — derive/reconstruct, explain factors/exponents/signs/index shifts, switch representations, test conditions.
- **ADOPT** — recognize in disguise, write the first move independently, reject a wrong method, transfer, rebuild.

`CONNECT` links concepts back to source IDs and forward to First-Step/JEE/question-bank items.
