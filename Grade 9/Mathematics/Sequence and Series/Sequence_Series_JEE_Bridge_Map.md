## Central user-supplied bridge anchors

### B1 — nested square sums

Surface form:

`1 + 1/2(1^2+2^2) + 1/3(1^2+2^2+3^2) + ...` up to 10 terms.

First-Step path:

`SEE nested accumulation -> WRITE T_n -> collapse inner sum -> split outer polynomial sum`.

Concept Book path:

Explain why `T_n = (1/n) sum_{k=1}^n k^2`, why the `n` cancels, and why summation linearity lets the resulting quadratic be split into standard sums.

### B2 — alternating cubes

Surface form:

`1^3 - 2^3 + 3^3 - 4^3 + ... + 15^3`.

First-Step path:

`SEE alternating parity -> CHOOSE odd/even split or adjacent pairing`.

Concept Book path:

Explain the equivalence of odd/even splitting and pairing, and connect the reduced expression to standard cube/polynomial sums.

## Hint-ladder policy

For each bridge item:

1. Hint 1 = recognition cue.
2. Hint 2 = representation/transformation.
3. Hint 3 = equation/setup.
4. Full arithmetic stays in the solution section.

The mastery target is fewer hints over time, not merely obtaining the final answer.

## Provenance rule

Retain year/shift metadata only when verified in the authoring source. Wording should be adapted/paraphrased for the learning product rather than reproduced as a facsimile. Keep external bridge provenance distinct from user-uploaded handwritten/source material.
