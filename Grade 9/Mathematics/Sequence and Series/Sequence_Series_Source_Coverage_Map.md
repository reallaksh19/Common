## Source QC items retained

Known handwritten transcription/QC points from the source review must not be silently lost:

- piecewise even sequence begins `-1/2, -1/4, -1/6, ...`;
- Fibonacci recurrence condition shown as `n > 2`;
- infinite-GP sum/squares example leads to `r = 3/5`;
- harmonic-means source endpoints are `2/3` and `2/13` before reciprocation;
- the HP 8th/14th-term example is unfinished in the handwriting;
- in the logarithmic AP problem the positive branch `2^x = 2/5` is not rejected; only the negative branch is impossible;
- handwritten infinite-GP condition is reproduced faithfully when transcribing source, while extension teaching may state the mathematically complete `|r| < 1` condition as verified outside-source clarification.
