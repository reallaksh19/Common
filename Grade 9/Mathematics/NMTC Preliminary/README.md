# NMTC Bhaskara Preliminary — Grade IX & X Mathematics

## Scope

This workspace is **Preliminary / Screening Test only** for the NMTC Junior (Bhaskara) level, Grades IX–X.

Stage II / Final preparation is out of scope for curriculum weighting, difficulty calibration, archetype frequency, mocks, and mastery thresholds.

## Learning objective

Build a student who can self-learn, self-evaluate, self-test, and apply mathematics under Preliminary conditions:

`SEE -> RECOGNIZE -> FIRST MOVE -> SOLVE EFFICIENTLY -> CHECK -> TRANSFER`

Deep concept teaching continues to use:

`SEE -> REALIZE -> UNDERSTAND -> ADOPT`

The Preliminary layer emphasizes recognition latency, first-move selection, compact reasoning, trap resistance and reliable checking.

## Grounding rule

Previous-year Bhaskara Preliminary papers are an authority layer, not an appendix.

No chapter is publishable until available relevant Preliminary questions have been:

1. source-classified;
2. assigned stable IDs;
3. fingerprinted at mathematical-mechanism level;
4. clustered into archetypes;
5. mapped to concept teaching;
6. represented by a foundation-to-PYQ ladder;
7. followed by non-identical transfer.

Historical frequency controls **practice emphasis**, never syllabus inclusion.
