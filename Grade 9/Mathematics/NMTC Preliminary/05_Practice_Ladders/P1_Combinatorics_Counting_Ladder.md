# P1 Combinatorics — F0 -> F4 -> PYQ -> XF Ladder

Each family follows:

`F0 prerequisite -> F1 direct -> F2 standard -> F3 disguised -> F4 Preliminary-speed -> PYQ -> XF transfer`

## Family A — Fundamental counting / order

- **F0:** list outcomes for two tiny choices.
- **F1:** multiply independent stages.
- **F2:** derive `nPr` and `nCr` from roles/order.
- **F3:** same objects but hidden role distinction.
- **F4:** classify ordered/unordered in <15 s.
- **PYQ:** use neighboring 2019/2025 counting anchors where structure matches.
- **XF:** mixed selection with one role distinguished and remaining group unordered.

## Family B — Restricted digit counts

- **F0:** place-value and leading-zero rule.
- **F1:** 3-digit even numbers without repetition.
- **F2:** divisibility by 5/9 with distinct digits.
- **F3:** restriction stated through digit sum or congruence.
- **F4:** choose controlling position/residue first.
- **PYQ:** `NMTC-BH-P-2025-Q21`.
- **XF:** four-digit count with parity + digit-sum congruence.

## Family C — Disjoint casework

- **F0:** split by parity.
- **F1:** split by last digit.
- **F2:** split configurations by structural type.
- **F3:** cases not given explicitly.
- **F4:** write case partition before arithmetic.
- **PYQ:** `NMTC-BH-P-2019-Q09` mechanism; source model helpful.
- **XF:** classify triangles from selected grid points by degeneracy/property.

## Family D — Complement counting

- **F0:** total minus forbidden.
- **F1:** strings containing at least one specified symbol.
- **F2:** arrangements avoiding a property.
- **F3:** “at least one repeat/adjacency” phrasing.
- **F4:** compare direct vs complement setup in <20 s.
- **PYQ:** syllabus-first; no strong clean direct anchor required.
- **XF:** count PINs containing at least one repeated category.

## Family E — Inclusion–exclusion

- **F0:** two overlapping sets.
- **F1:** divisibility by 2 or 3.
- **F2:** two restrictions on finite integers.
- **F3:** three-set overlap.
- **F4:** write signs before substituting counts.
- **PYQ:** syllabus-first author-created.
- **XF:** count `1..N` satisfying at least one of three divisibility conditions.

## Family F — Pigeonhole

- **F0:** more objects than boxes.
- **F1:** same remainder modulo `m`.
- **F2:** guarantee at least `ceil(N/k)` occupancy.
- **F3:** boxes must be invented from intervals/pairs/residues.
- **F4:** identify pigeons/boxes/forced occupancy in <20 s.
- **PYQ:** syllabus-first author-created.
- **XF:** select integers from a range and force close pair / same residue.

## Family G — Subset-product expansion

- **F0:** expand `(1+a)(1+b)`.
- **F1:** map terms to subsets.
- **F2:** sum non-empty subset products.
- **F3:** rational/geometric values telescope after product formation.
- **F4:** recognize `product(1+a_i)-1` immediately.
- **PYQ:** `NMTC-BH-P-2019-Q07`.
- **XF:** weighted subset sum/product with a new finite sequence.

## Family H — Coefficient as count

- **F0:** coefficient of `x^k` in two short sums.
- **F1:** bounded pair count `i+j=k`.
- **F2:** asymmetric exponent bounds.
- **F3:** three-factor bounded tuple count.
- **F4:** translate coefficient to integer equation before expansion.
- **PYQ:** `NMTC-BH-P-2019-Q30`.
- **XF:** coefficient count with parity or skipped exponents.

## Family I — State/path counting

- **F0:** count 2-step walks on 3 states.
- **F1:** recurrence table by time and state.
- **F2:** forbidden boundary/state.
- **F3:** exact-length path on graph.
- **F4:** define state transition before listing paths.
- **PYQ:** `NMTC-BH-P-2019-Q23` figure-gated.
- **XF:** text-complete line/cycle graph.

## Family J — High-ceiling representation count

- **F0:** binary include/exclude representation.
- **F1:** signed coefficient choices.
- **F2:** uniqueness of powers-of-base representation.
- **F3:** constrain sign/non-negativity/number of nonzero coefficients.
- **F4:** prove uniqueness before counting cases.
- **PYQ:** `NMTC-BH-P-2019-Q28` high-ceiling bridge.
- **XF:** balanced representation with smaller powers/base.

## Promotion rule

Do not promote a learner to F4/PYQ/XF merely because they can evaluate `nPr`/`nCr`. They must first classify the outcome object and explain why order/overlap restrictions justify the chosen model.
