# P0 Algebra — Inequalities / Bounds / Equality Practice Ladder

## Ladder rule

Each family follows:

`F0 FOUNDATION -> F1 DIRECT -> F2 DISGUISED -> F3 MIXED -> F4 PRELIMINARY SPEED -> PYQ -> XF TRANSFER`

The student must state the first move before solving from F2 upward.

---

# Family A — Boundedness first

### F0
For positive `t`, inspect `t+1/t` as `t` becomes very large.

### F1
Given `ab=1`, decide whether `a+b` has a minimum and/or maximum.

### F2
Given `xy=9`, decide whether `x^2+y^2` has a maximum over positive reals.

### F3
Given `abc=1`, decide whether `a+b+c` has a maximum over positive reals.

### F4
A problem asks for the maximum of a symmetric positive expression under a product constraint. Write only the boundedness test/family, not an inequality.

### PYQ
`NMTC-BH-P-2023-Q17` — unbounded maximum contrast.

### XF
Change product, number of variables and target power; preserve the need to falsify a maximum by an escaping family.

---

# Family B — AM-GM + equality

### F0
Prove `(a+b)/2 >= sqrt(ab)` from `(sqrt a-sqrt b)^2>=0`.

### F1
Positive `a,b` satisfy `ab=25`. Find minimum `a+b`.

### F2
Positive `x,y` satisfy `x+y=14`. Find maximum `xy`.

### F3
Four positive numbers have product `16` and sum `8`. What does equality tell you?

### F4
A monic quartic has four positive roots with known product and root sum exactly equal to the AM-GM minimum. Infer the roots without solving the quartic.

### PYQ
`NMTC-BH-P-2024-Q17`.

### XF
Alter product/sum while preserving equality collapse into repeated roots.

# Family C — Cauchy / reciprocal constraints

### F0

### F1

### F2
If `1/x+9/y=1`, find the least possible `x+y`.

### F3

### F4
Recognize a reciprocal constraint whose coefficients are perfect squares and write the Cauchy first line immediately.

### PYQ
`NMTC-BH-P-2018-Q12`.

### XF
Change reciprocal weights and right-hand constant; preserve proportionality/equality reasoning.
