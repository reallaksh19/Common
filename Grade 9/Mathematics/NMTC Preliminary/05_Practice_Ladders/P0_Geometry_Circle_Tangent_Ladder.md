# P0 Geometry — Circle & Tangent Practice Ladders

Each family uses:

`F0 prerequisite -> F1 direct -> F2 standard -> F3 disguised -> F4 Preliminary-speed -> PYQ evidence -> XF transfer`

Historical IDs are mechanism anchors only where source custody permits; figure-gated IDs are not redrawn here.

---

## Ladder A — radius perpendicular to tangent

- **F0:** identify radius and tangent point.
- **F1:** given OA radius and tangent l at A, state `OA⊥l`.
- **F2:** use the right angle to find a triangle angle.
- **F3:** combine with isosceles radii.
- **F4:** tangent + parallel + one unknown angle.
- **PYQ evidence:** 2024 Q03, 2025 Q11 (`FIGURE_GATED`).
- **XF:** tangent point hidden until equal lengths identify the radius triangle.

## Ladder B — equal tangents

- **F0:** prove `PA=PB` from two right triangles.
- **F1:** solve direct length.
- **F2:** infer isosceles angles.
- **F3:** combine with chord/cyclic relation.
- **F4:** tangent equality plus similarity/metric relation.
- **PYQ evidence:** 2024/2025 tangent families; exact figure custody varies.
- **XF:** perimeter or ratio problem where tangent equality is not named.

## Ladder C — centre angle / inscribed angle

- **F0:** identify same arc endpoints.
- **F1:** central 100° -> circumference 50°.
- **F2:** circumference 37° -> central 74°.
- **F3:** mix with triangle isosceles angle.
- **F4:** one short circle chain.
- **PYQ evidence:** 2023 Q11 (`FIGURE_GATED`).
- **XF:** prove an angle without being told the relevant arc.

## Ladder D — same segment / cyclic quadrilateral

- **F0:** identify same chord.
- **F1:** equal angles in same segment.
- **F2:** opposite cyclic angle from supplement.
- **F3:** decide whether equality or supplement is the correct relation.
- **F4:** isosceles + cyclic chain.
- **PYQ evidence:** 2023 Q22 (`FIGURE_GATED`).
- **XF:** prove four points cyclic from an angle equality, then continue.

## Ladder E — diameter / semicircle

- **F0:** diameter corresponds to 180° at centre.
- **F1:** angle in semicircle = 90°.
- **F2:** use Pythagoras.
- **F3:** use altitude/right-triangle similarity.
- **F4:** tangent + semicircle metric.
- **PYQ evidence:** 2024 Q18/Q25/Q29 (`FIGURE_GATED`).
- **XF:** recover a radius from a rectangle/square around a semicircle.

## Ladder F — alternate segment theorem

- **F0:** identify tangent point and chord endpoints.
- **F1:** copy tangent-chord angle to remote angle.
- **F2:** combine with same-segment equality.
- **F3:** add a parallel-line transfer.
- **F4:** short mixed tangent/cyclic chase.
- **PYQ evidence:** 2024 Q08 (`FIGURE_GATED`); syllabus-required regardless of exact recurrence.
- **XF:** prove a triangle is isosceles from tangent-chord information.

## Ladder G — intersecting chords / power

- **F0:** distinguish internal intersection from external secants.
- **F1:** `XA·XB=XC·XD` direct numeric solve.
- **F2:** tangent-secant `PT²=PA·PB`.
- **F3:** combine with Pythagoras or similarity.
- **F4:** target hidden as a square/product.
- **PYQ evidence:** 2023 Q05 is starred/source-conflicted; 2025 Q25 is figure-gated metric evidence.
- **XF:** determine which product theorem applies before numbers are used.

## Ladder H — tangent + parallel transfer

- **F0:** corresponding/alternate angles with parallels.
- **F1:** transfer tangent angle to a chord angle.
- **F2:** invoke alternate-segment relation.
- **F3:** combine with equal tangents.
- **F4:** solve a multi-relation angle target in 3–4 moves.
- **PYQ evidence:** 2024 Q08, 2025 Q28 (`FIGURE_GATED`).
- **XF:** parallel is not adjacent to target; student must transfer twice.

## Ladder I — tangent-circle centre distance / scale

- **F0:** external tangency -> sum of radii; internal -> difference.
- **F1:** solve one centre distance.
- **F2:** circles tangent to both sides of an angle: centres on angle bisector.
- **F3:** infer scale ratio from radii.
- **F4:** geometric progression of tangent-circle radii.
- **PYQ evidence:** 2024 Q13; 2019 Q21 figure-gated.
- **XF:** recover an unknown radius from two tangent constraints.

## Ladder J — source integrity in geometry

- **F0:** recognize missing diagram as missing data.
- **F1:** distinguish theorem mechanism from exact historical geometry.
- **F2:** identify target-label contradiction.
- **F3:** classify `FIGURE_GATED` vs `SOURCE_CONFLICT`.
- **F4:** solve a clean author-created analogue while refusing to claim it is the historical diagram.
- **PYQ evidence:** 2023 Q05/Q06 and multiple figure-gated 2019–2025 items.
- **XF:** given a solution paragraph with no figure, list what cannot be reconstructed safely.

## Ladder completion rule

A family is adopted only when the learner can:

1. name the first forced mark/relation;
2. justify why it applies to the same chord/arc/tangent configuration;
3. solve an F4 item without theorem labels;
4. solve a surface-changed XF item;
5. avoid using visual scale as evidence.
