# Circle & Tangent Recognition — PYQ Source Coverage Map

## Authority rule

This map records mechanism evidence, not permission to recreate missing diagrams.

A historical item is classified as:

- `CLEAN_TEXT_OR_RETAINED_FIGURE` — mechanism can support a canonical teaching anchor;
- `FIGURE_GATED` — answer/solution exists but exact source diagram is not yet retained in this workspace;
- `SOURCE_CONFLICT` — labels/stem/option custody is inconsistent;
- `STAR_OR_BONUS` — useful ceiling evidence but not ordinary scored recurrence.

## Syllabus-first requirement

The 2026 syllabus explicitly requires circle theorems, chords/arcs, cyclic quadrilaterals, tangents, intersecting chord theorem and alternate segment theorem. These are taught even where the currently qualified five-year corpus has incomplete exact-figure custody.

## Publication consequence

The Concept Book and author-created transfer items may teach every theorem above. A historical figure-dependent question may be referenced by stable ID and mechanism, but its exact diagram/stem is not reproduced until source custody is complete.
