## Editorial findings locked

1. Use `sqrt(x^2)=|x|`, never silently `x`.
2. Every real logarithm exercise must retain base/argument validity where material.
3. When the whole repeated object is `sqrt(log_b x)`, substitution should preserve the nonnegative condition.
4. Squared radical equations require checking in the original equation.
5. Cubing over the reals is one-to-one, but source wording about “roots” vs algebraic multiplicity must not be silently reinterpreted.
6. Do not state a log law for sums or differences.
7. Exact inverse structures should be simplified before decimal approximation.
