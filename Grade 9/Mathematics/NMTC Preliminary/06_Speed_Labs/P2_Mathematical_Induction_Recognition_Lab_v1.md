For each prompt, identify the correct first-move code without completing the proof.

Codes: `PN BC SA PF DV IQ RC SI BR DP QC`.

1. Prove `1+3+...+(2n-1)=n^2`.
2. Claim valid only for `n>=4`.
3. Prove `7 | (8^n-1)`.
4. Verify a proposed formula for `a_{n+1}=3a_n+2`.
5. Prove `2^n>=n+1`.
6. A proof assumes `P(k+1)`.
7. A statement is obviously `n(n-1)(n+1)` after factorization.
8. The next case depends on two previous recurrence values.
9. A product formula adds one new factor at `k+1`.
10. A webpage calls an exercise “official NMTC induction” without source ID.
11. Proof shows `P(k)->P(k+2)` and only checks `P(1)`.
12. Need to state the proposition and domain before starting.
13. Sum through k becomes sum through k+1.
14. Inequality proof uses IH but still needs `2(k+1)>=k+2`.
15. Strong hypothesis assumes all cases up to k.
16. Base case chosen as 1 though the theorem begins at 3.
17. Divisibility next-case expression can be rewritten as `aF(k)+mC`.
18. A finite product telescopes immediately without induction.
19. Recurrence formula has already been derived and only needs verification.
20. A polished proof changes the proposition from equality to inequality midway.
