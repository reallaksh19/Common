# P0 Geometry — Circle & Tangent Recognition Lab v1

## Purpose

Recognition only. Do not solve.

Suggested internal drill: 20 items, 10–20 seconds each. This is not an official NMTC timing claim.

Use codes:

- `RT` radius⊥tangent
- `ET` equal tangents
- `CI` centre/inscribed
- `SS` same segment
- `DI` diameter right angle
- `CY` cyclic supplementary/converse
- `AS` alternate segment
- `IC` intersecting chords
- `PP` power of point outside
- `PA` parallel transfer first
- `TC` tangent-circle centre distance/scale
- `MG` metric right-triangle/similarity
- `QC` figure/source custody

## Items

1. Tangent at A, centre O visible.
2. Two tangents PA,PB from external P.
3. Central angle AOB and angle ACB on same arc AB.
4. Two angles subtend chord AB from same segment.
5. AB is a diameter; C lies on circle.
6. ABCD is cyclic and one opposite angle is known.
7. Opposite angles of a simple quadrilateral sum to 180°; decide a circle conclusion.
8. Tangent at A and chord AB; remote angle on circle uses endpoints A,B.
9. Chords AB and CD meet at X inside the circle.
10. Tangent PT and secant P-A-B from external P.
11. Two secants from P, with near and far intersection points given.
12. Tangent/chord angle plus a line parallel to that chord.
13. Two circles externally tangent; radii given.
14. Two circles internally tangent; radii given.
15. Diameter creates a right triangle and two sides are given.
16. Tangent radius creates a right triangle with OP known.
17. A historical solution mentions a marked parallel line, but the original figure is absent.
18. A historical question label conflicts with a length already assigned to that segment.
19. A learner uses `external×whole` for chords intersecting inside.
20. A learner measures 50° from a diagram marked not-to-scale.
