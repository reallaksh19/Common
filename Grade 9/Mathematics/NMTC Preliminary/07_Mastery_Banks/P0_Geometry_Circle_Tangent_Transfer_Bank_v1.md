# Family A — tangent radius / equal tangents

## A1
A circle has centre O and radius 5. From an external point P, PT is tangent at T and `OP=13`. Find PT.

**Answer:** `12`.

**Solution:** `OT⊥PT`; right triangle OPT gives `PT=sqrt(13²-5²)=12`.

**Profile:** `3/4/3/3/2/4/1/2/3/3`.

## A2
PT is tangent to a circle at T, O is the centre, and `∠OPT=34°`. Find `∠POT`.

**Answer:** `56°`.

**Solution:** `∠OTP=90°`; triangle sum.

**Profile:** `2/3/2/2/1/3/1/1/2/2`.

## A3
From P, tangents PA and PB touch a circle. If `PA=8` and `AB=10`, find the perimeter of triangle PAB.

**Answer:** `26`.

**Solution:** `PB=PA=8`; perimeter `8+8+10`.

**Profile:** `2/3/2/2/1/3/1/1/2/2`.

# Family B — centre / same segment / cyclic

## B1
`∠AOB=136°` is a central angle. C lies on the circle on the corresponding arc so that `∠ACB` subtends chord AB. Find `∠ACB`.

**Answer:** `68°`.

**Solution:** inscribed angle is half the central angle on the same arc.

**Profile:** `2/3/2/2/1/3/1/1/2/2`.

## B2
C and D lie on the same segment of a circle. If `∠ACB=47°` and both angles `∠ACB,∠ADB` subtend chord AB, find `∠ADB`.

**Answer:** `47°`.

**Solution:** angles in the same segment are equal.

**Profile:** `2/3/2/2/1/3/1/1/3/2`.

## B3
ABCD is cyclic and `∠A=118°`. Find `∠C`.

**Answer:** `62°`.

**Solution:** opposite cyclic angles are supplementary.

**Profile:** `2/3/2/2/1/3/1/1/2/2`.

# Family C — tangent-chord / alternate segment

## C1
A tangent at A makes an angle of `38°` with chord AB. C is a point on the circle in the alternate segment. Find `∠ACB`.

**Answer:** `38°`.

**Solution:** alternate-segment theorem for chord AB.

**Profile:** `3/5/4/3/1/5/1/1/4/4`.

## C2
C lies on a circle and `∠ACB=52°`. The tangent at A meets chord AB. Find the acute angle between the tangent and chord AB.

**Answer:** `52°`.

**Solution:** converse use of the tangent-chord relation.

**Profile:** `3/5/4/3/1/5/1/1/4/4`.

## C3
A tangent at A and chord AB form an angle of `42°`. C is on the alternate segment and `AC=BC`. Find `∠BAC`.

**Answer:** `69°`.

**Solution:** alternate segment gives `∠ACB=42°`. Since `AC=BC`, base angles A and B are equal: `(180-42)/2=69`.

**Profile:** `4/6/5/4/2/6/2/2/5/5`.

# Family D — intersecting chords / power of a point

## D1
Chords AB and CD intersect at X inside a circle. `XA=3`, `XB=8`, `XC=4`. Find XD.

**Answer:** `6`.

**Solution:** `3·8=4·XD`.

**Profile:** `3/4/3/2/2/4/1/2/3/3`.

## D2
From external point P, PT is tangent to a circle and P-A-B is a secant with `PA=8`. If `PT=12`, find `PB`.

**Answer:** `18`.

**Solution:** `PT²=PA·PB`; `144=8PB`.

**Profile:** `4/5/4/3/2/5/2/2/4/4`.

## D3
From external point P, secants P-A-B and P-C-D meet a circle. `PA=4`, `PB=15`, `PC=6`. Find PD.

**Answer:** `10`.

**Solution:** `PA·PB=PC·PD`; `60=6PD`.

**Profile:** `4/5/4/3/2/5/2/2/4/4`.

# Family E — diameter / tangent-circle metric

## E1
AB is a diameter of a circle of length 10. C lies on the circle and `AC=6`. Find BC.

**Answer:** `8`.

**Solution:** `∠ACB=90°`; Pythagoras: `BC=sqrt(100-36)=8`.

**Profile:** `3/4/3/3/2/4/1/2/3/3`.

## E2
Two circles of radii 5 and 8 are externally tangent. Find the distance between their centres.

**Answer:** `13`.

**Solution:** centres and tangency point are collinear; distance is sum of radii.

**Profile:** `2/3/2/2/1/3/1/1/2/2`.

## E3
Two circles of radii 8 and 5 are internally tangent. Find the distance between their centres.

**Answer:** `3`.

**Solution:** internal tangent centre distance is the radius difference.

**Profile:** `2/4/3/2/1/4/2/1/4/3`.

# Family F — theorem choice / cyclic converse / source QC

## F1
A quadrilateral has opposite angles `113°` and `67°`. What circle-related conclusion is available, assuming the vertices form a simple quadrilateral?

**Answer:** it is cyclic.

**Solution:** opposite angles are supplementary; use the converse cyclic criterion.

**Profile:** `3/5/4/3/1/5/2/1/4/4`.

## F2
A learner sees chords AB and CD intersecting at X **inside** a circle and writes `XA·AB=XC·CD`. Diagnose the error and write the correct relation.

**Answer:** they used whole-chord lengths instead of the segments made by X. Correct: `XA·XB=XC·XD`.

**Profile:** `4/6/5/3/1/6/2/1/6/4`.
