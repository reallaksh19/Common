# P1 Geometry — Triangle Metric First-Step Cards

Use these as recognition-only cards. Do not solve before naming the first relation.

## Card 1 — Median from three sides
Trigger: midpoint + all three side lengths.
First move: Apollonius.

## Card 2 — General cevian
Trigger: side split `m,n` + three outer sides/cevian target.
First move: Stewart with explicit label map.

## Card 3 — Altitude cancellation
Trigger: same altitude appears in two Pythagorean triangles and target is a difference of side squares.
First move: subtract the two Pythagorean equations.

## Card 4 — Angle bisector position
Trigger: `AD` bisects angle `A`.
First move: `BD/DC = AB/AC`.

## Card 5 — Angle bisector length
Trigger: angle bisector length required.
First move: angle-bisector ratio for `BD,DC`, then Stewart.

## Card 6 — Perpendicular medians
Trigger: medians + perpendicularity.
First move: consider vectors/coordinates before scalar theorem expansion.

## Card 7 — Midpoint but no median length asked
Trigger: midpoint gives equal base parts but target may be side relation.
First move: specialize Stewart/Apollonius to eliminate the median.

## Card 8 — Right triangle + circumradius
Trigger: right triangle and `R`.
First move: hypotenuse `=2R`.

## Card 9 — Right triangle + inradius
Trigger: right triangle and `r`.
First move: `p+q-h=2r`.

## Card 10 — `R:r` ratio
Trigger: right triangle + radius ratio.
First move: normalize hypotenuse via `2R`, then use leg sum/product.

## Card 11 — Cevian label safety
Trigger: Stewart substitution.
First move: write `BD=m, DC=n, AB=c, AC=b` before numbers.
Trap: swapping which side square multiplies which base part.

## Card 12 — Cosine-law alternative
Trigger: included angle + two adjacent sides, no useful side split.
First move: cosine law may be cheaper than introducing Stewart.

## Card 13 — Source-gated geometry
Trigger: historical problem depends on missing/inconsistent figure/data.
First move: stop canonicalization; retain only mechanism-level evidence.

## Card 14 — Theorem reconstruction
Trigger: formula forgotten.
First move: drop an altitude and build two Pythagorean equations; derive the needed Stewart/Apollonius case.

# Contrast pairs

- median vs angle bisector;
- median vs general cevian;
- altitude subtraction vs Stewart;
- Apollonius vs coordinate/vector route;
- right-triangle metric identities vs trigonometric half-angle formulas;
- correct theorem route vs source-conflicted numerical claim.

# Success criterion

On an unlabeled prompt, identify the best first relation within 15–20 seconds and justify it in one sentence.
