# P0 Geometry — Circle & Tangent First-Step Cards

Use these cards to decide the first useful mark/relation before solving.

## Card 1 — Tangent point visible

**SEE:** tangent touches circle at A; centre O is known.

**WRITE:** `OA ⟂ tangent at A`.

**WHY:** radius to point of contact is perpendicular to tangent.

**DO NOT:** assume any other radius is perpendicular to the tangent.

---

## Card 2 — Two tangents from one external point

**SEE:** P-A and P-B are tangents.

**WRITE:** `PA=PB`.

**WHY:** right triangles with common hypotenuse OP and equal radii are congruent.

**NEXT:** mark isosceles consequences or replace tangent lengths algebraically.

---

## Card 3 — Centre angle and circumference angle on same arc

**SEE:** `∠AOB` at centre and `∠ACB` at circle use endpoints A,B.

**WRITE:** `∠AOB=2∠ACB`.

**CHECK:** same chord/arc endpoints.

---

## Card 4 — Same chord seen from two points

**SEE:** `∠ACB` and `∠ADB` both subtend chord AB from same segment.

**WRITE:** `∠ACB=∠ADB`.

**TRAP:** if C,D are on opposite segments, do not use equality blindly.

---

## Card 5 — Diameter present

**SEE:** AB is diameter; C lies on circle.

**WRITE:** `∠ACB=90°`.

**NEXT:** Pythagoras/similarity often becomes available.

---

## Card 6 — Four points known cyclic

**SEE:** A,B,C,D lie on one circle.

**WRITE:** `∠A+∠C=180°` and `∠B+∠D=180°`.

**NEXT:** combine with isosceles/parallel/triangle facts.

---

## Card 7 — Tangent + chord angle

**SEE:** tangent at A and chord AB; target angle is elsewhere on circle.

**WRITE:** angle(tangent AB, chord AB) = angle in alternate segment subtended by AB.

**CHECK:** preserve chord endpoints A,B in the remote angle.

---

## Card 8 — Chords intersect inside circle

**SEE:** AB and CD intersect at X inside.

**WRITE:** `XA·XB=XC·XD`.

**WHY:** similarity of triangles formed by equal subtended angles.

**TRAP:** do not use external-secant segment conventions here.

---

## Card 9 — Two secants from external point

**SEE:** P-A-B and P-C-D are secants from P, near points A,C.

**WRITE:** `PA·PB=PC·PD`.

**REMEMBER:** external × whole.

---

## Card 10 — Tangent + secant from external point

**SEE:** PT tangent; P-A-B secant.

**WRITE:** `PT²=PA·PB`.

**TRAP:** not `PT=PA·PB`.

---

## Card 11 — Tangent plus parallel line

**SEE:** tangent/chord angle and a parallel line.

**WRITE:** transfer the angle through parallelism first.

**THEN:** apply tangent-chord/same-segment/cyclic relation.

---

## Card 12 — Tangent circles

**SEE:** two circles tangent internally/externally.

**WRITE:** join centres through the point of tangency.

**DISTANCE:** external tangency -> `O1O2=r1+r2`; internal tangency -> `O1O2=|r1-r2|`.

---

## Card 13 — Circle metric problem

**SEE:** radius/tangent/diameter creates right angle.

**WRITE:** right triangle first; only then Pythagoras/similarity/power.

**TRAP:** do not coordinate-bash before marking forced geometry.

---

## Card 14 — Diagram missing or label conflict

**SEE:** answer/solution exists but exact diagram is missing, or target label conflicts with given data.

**WRITE:** `FIGURE_GATED` or `SOURCE_CONFLICT`.

**ACTION:** teach the mechanism with an author-created clean problem; do not recreate the historical diagram as exact.

## Adoption standard

Student is ready only if they can choose the correct card from an unlabelled diagram/text before calculating.
