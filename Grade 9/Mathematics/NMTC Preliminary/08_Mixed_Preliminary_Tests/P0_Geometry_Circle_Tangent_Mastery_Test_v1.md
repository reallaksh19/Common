# Student paper

## Q1
A circle has centre O and radius 7. From P, PT is tangent at T and `OP=25`. Find PT.

## Q2
From P, PA and PB are tangents to a circle. `PA=9` and `AB=14`. Find the perimeter of triangle PAB.

## Q3
`∠AOB=150°` is a central angle and `∠ACB` subtends the same chord AB. Find `∠ACB`.

## Q4
ABCD is cyclic and `∠A=126°`. Find `∠C`.

## Q5
A tangent at A makes an angle of `36°` with chord AB. C lies in the alternate segment and `AC=BC`. Find `∠BAC`.

## Q6
Chords AB and CD intersect at X inside a circle. `XA=4`, `XB=9`, `XC=6`. Find XD.

## Q7
From P, PT is tangent and P-A-B is a secant. `PT=15`, `PA=9`. Find PB.

## Q8
From P, secants P-A-B and P-C-D meet a circle. `PA=5`, `PB=20`, `PC=8`. Find PD.

## Q9
AB is a diameter of length 26 and C lies on the circle. If `AC=10`, find BC.

## Q10
Two circles of radii 6 and 11 are externally tangent. Find the distance between their centres.

## Q11
A tangent at A makes `44°` with chord AB. Through B, line l is drawn parallel to the tangent. C lies on the circle in the alternate segment. Find:

`(angle between BA and l) + ∠ACB`.

## Q12 — source custody

A historical answer key gives `80°`. A secondary solution says “use the tangent and the marked parallel line,” but the exact diagram is missing from the retained corpus. What is the correct publication action?

# Answer / review section

## Q1
**Code:** `RT` + `MG`.

`OT⊥PT`, so triangle OPT is right.

`PT=sqrt(25²-7²)=sqrt576=24`.

**Answer:** `24`.

## Q2
**Code:** `ET`.

`PB=PA=9`.

Perimeter `=9+9+14=32`.

**Answer:** `32`.

## Q3
**Code:** `CI`.

Inscribed angle on same arc is half central angle.

**Answer:** `75°`.

## Q4
**Code:** `CY`.

Opposite cyclic angles are supplementary.

`∠C=180-126=54°`.

**Answer:** `54°`.

## Q5
**Code:** `AS`.

Alternate segment gives `∠ACB=36°`.

Since `AC=BC`, triangle ABC has equal base angles A and B:

`∠BAC=(180-36)/2=72°`.

**Answer:** `72°`.

## Q6
**Code:** `IC`.

`XA·XB=XC·XD`.

`4·9=6·XD`, so `XD=6`.

**Answer:** `6`.

## Q7
**Code:** `PP`.

`PT²=PA·PB`.

`225=9PB`, hence `PB=25`.

**Answer:** `25`.

## Q8
**Code:** `PP`.

`PA·PB=PC·PD`.

`5·20=8PD`.

`PD=25/2`.

**Answer:** `25/2`.

## Q9
**Code:** `DI` + `MG`.

Angle ACB is 90°.

`BC=sqrt(26²-10²)=sqrt576=24`.

**Answer:** `24`.

## Q10
**Code:** `TC`.

For external tangency, centre distance is sum of radii.

**Answer:** `17`.

## Q11
**Code:** `PA` + `AS`.

Because `l` is parallel to the tangent, the acute angle between BA and l equals the tangent-chord angle `44°`.

By alternate-segment theorem, `∠ACB=44°`.

Sum `=88°`.

**Answer:** `88°`.

## Q12
**Code:** `QC`.

The exact historical geometry is not recoverable from the answer and prose alone.

**Answer:** mark the item `FIGURE_GATED`; do not publish a guessed reconstruction as the exact PYQ. Teach the mechanism with a clearly labelled author-created analogue until exact figure custody is obtained.
