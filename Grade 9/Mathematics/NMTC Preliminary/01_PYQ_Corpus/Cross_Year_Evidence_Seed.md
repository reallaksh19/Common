## Mechanism recurrence already visible

The following remain **candidate recurring families** across the five-year seed. Exact scored counts wait for 2023–2025 qualification and bonus/image handling.

### 1. Transform before calculate — very strong

Observed through low-degree power reduction, radical/common-basis simplification, reciprocal linearization, logarithmic substitution, exponential normalization, identity collapse and function transformations.

Curriculum consequence: every algebra chapter needs explicit `TRANSFORM` and `FIRST MOVE` training.

### 2. Polynomial/root structure — very strong

Observed families include factor/remainder reduction, polynomial divisibility, common roots, Vieta, integer-root restrictions, repeated-root conditions and coefficient matching.

Curriculum consequence: Quadratics, higher-degree equations, factor/remainder theorem and Vieta should be connected structurally.

### 3. Radical / exponent / logarithm compression — strong

Radical and exponential transformations recur; explicit logarithm problems are visible in recent reproductions.

Curriculum consequence: include recognition cards for common base, conjugate, `t+1/t`, logarithmic variable and nested-radical forms.

### 4. Remainder / divisibility / modular thinking — strong

Observed through common-remainder structures, modular powers, last-digit cycles, polynomial remainders, digit divisibility, integer-valued rational expressions and prime-order filters.

2019 Q26 materially strengthens the need for a higher-ceiling modular/order bridge: it asks for the least odd prime divisor of `2019^8+1` and uses order/congruence reasoning.

### 5. Geometry recognition chains — strong

Every ingested year contains short objective geometry requiring rapid recognition: tangent/radius/diameter, cyclic/circle-angle chains, square/rhombus structures, equal-distance geometry, medians/altitudes, and tangent-circle center geometry.

Many questions require figures. Geometry publication remains gated on exact figure recovery even where answer/solution evidence is known.

### 6. Sequence/accumulation structures — recurring

Observed through recurrences, consecutive block sums, triangular-number differences, GP recovery, infinite-GP constraints and functional sequence rules.

2019 Q20 is a useful concept example but is BONUS and must not inflate scored recurrence. Q29 is a scored functional-recurrence anchor.

### 7. Counting appears in disguised forms

Observed through digit constraints, connected shapes, exact-length paths, subset-product expansion, geometric counting, balanced ternary and coefficient convolution.

Curriculum consequence: Fundamental Counting Principle must include modeling/case construction and generating-product/coefficient viewpoints, not only `nPr/nCr` formulas.

## Source-quality lessons promoted to policy

1. **Truncation is a source defect.** Missing questions at the end of a secondary page are not evidence of exam length.
2. **Bonus disposition matters.** A mathematically valid question may not be a scored frequency datum.
3. **Image solution recovery is not image custody.** Knowing an answer does not authorize reconstruction of a missing diagram.
4. **Independent matching can resolve transcription without pretending the source is official.** P1 is distinct from P0.
5. **Never silently repair.** Every correction must carry its before/after evidence trail.
