## Six lenses

- `POSITION` — indexing, nth term, recurrence.
- `CHANGE` — finite differences and AP.
- `RATIO` — GP and repeated multiplication.
- `ACCUMULATION` — series, `S_n`, sigma and sums.
- `TRANSFORM` — reciprocal/HP, pairing, splitting, logarithmic transform, telescoping.
- `REVERSE` — `a_n = S_n - S_{n-1}`.

## Summation exemplar

Teach `1 + 2 + 3 + 4 + 5` before `sum_{k=1}^5 k`.

Interpret sigma as ADD + counter + START + STOP + term generator. Train both expansion and compression. Only then introduce `sum 1`, `sum k`, `sum k^2`, `sum k^3`, splitting, nested sums, alternating powers and telescoping.

## Central JEE transfer anchors

- `1^3 - 2^3 + 3^3 - ... + 15^3` — odd/even split or adjacent pairing.
- `1 + 1/2(1^2+2^2) + 1/3(1^2+2^2+3^2) + ...` to 10 terms — write the general term, collapse the inner square sum, split the outer polynomial sum.

The First-Step product teaches recognition/hints; the Concept Book teaches why the transformation works.
