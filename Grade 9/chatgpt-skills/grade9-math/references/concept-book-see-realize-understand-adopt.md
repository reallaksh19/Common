# Grade 9 Mathematics Concept Book Protocol

Use this protocol when a Mathematics concept/reference book must teach how to see and own the structure, not merely list formulas.

Required cognitive sequence:

`SEE -> REALIZE -> UNDERSTAND -> ADOPT`

`CONNECT` is source traceability/navigation, not a fifth cognitive stage.

## SEE

Begin with a visible mathematical object: number pattern, table, finite differences, ratio marks, diagram, graph, worked fragment, repeated operation, expansion, or contrast pair. Do not introduce an important formula as an unexplained starting point.

## REALIZE

Identify what stays structurally unchanged or which transformation makes the problem simpler. Typical structures include constant difference, constant ratio, recurrence, symmetry, accumulation, reciprocal structure, cancellation, parity, factorization, equal spacing, and equal scaling.

## UNDERSTAND

Rebuild the mathematics. Explain the origin of factors, exponents, signs and index shifts; connect equivalent representations; test boundary/special cases; confront plausible wrong methods; and show why transformations preserve the problem.

## ADOPT

Require independent use in a changed surface form. Each major concept should include several of: `RECOGNIZE`, `FIRST MOVE`, `WHY NOT?`, `TRANSFER`, and `REBUILD`.

Adoption means the learner can choose the structure without being told the chapter label.

## Mathematical lenses

For Sequence & Series use:

- `POSITION` — what lives at term n?
- `CHANGE` — what repeats additively?
- `RATIO` — what repeats multiplicatively?
- `ACCUMULATION` — what happens when terms are added?
- `TRANSFORM` — can the appearance be changed into an easier structure?
- `REVERSE` — can one term be recovered from cumulative information?

## Summation teaching contract

Teach sigma notation as compressed repeated addition:

`long addition -> counter/index -> start/stop -> expand -> compress -> split -> standard sums -> hidden transformations`.

Establish ordinary addition before sigma notation, then prove the behavior of splitting/linearity through expanded terms before using it symbolically.
