## Equation passports

### EP-M01 — Displacement

`Delta x = x_f - x_i`

**SEE:** number line with start and finish positions.

**REALIZE:** displacement records change in position, not path length.

**UNDERSTAND:** sign communicates direction; closed journeys can have zero displacement and positive distance; `|Delta x| <= distance`.

Source links: Q1-Q5.

### EP-M02 — Average speed

`average speed = total distance / total time`

**SEE:** two equal road lengths travelled at different speeds.

**REALIZE:** the slower segment occupies more time, so equal-distance average speed is not generally the arithmetic mean.

**UNDERSTAND:** contrast equal-time and equal-distance cases; derive the equal-distance harmonic form only after the definition is secure.

Source links: Q6-Q10, especially Q8-Q9.

### EP-M03 — Acceleration

`a = (v - u) / t`

**SEE:** velocity table such as 10, 12, 14, 16 m/s at successive seconds.

**REALIZE:** acceleration says how much velocity changes per unit time.

**UNDERSTAND:** acceleration can be positive, negative, or non-zero even when instantaneous speed is zero; distinguish speed change from velocity change.

Source links: Q11-Q14, Q28-Q31.

### EP-M04 — First constant-acceleration relation

`v = u + at`

**SEE:** accumulated equal velocity gains each second.

**REALIZE:** final velocity equals starting velocity plus all velocity gained during the interval.

**UNDERSTAND:** derive from acceleration definition; check units; if `a = 0`, recover `v = u`; connect to a straight-line `v-t` graph.

Source links: Q11, Q12, Q14, Q22, Q31 and gravity applications.

### EP-M05 — Displacement under constant acceleration

`s = ut + 1/2 at^2`

**SEE:** velocity-time area split into rectangle plus triangle.

**REALIZE:** displacement is initial-velocity motion plus extra displacement built by acceleration.

**UNDERSTAND:** explain why `1/2` and `t^2` appear; derive via graph/average velocity; check dimensions and the `a = 0` limit.

Source links: Q13, Q16-Q17, Q20, Q24, Q26-Q27, Q32-Q34 and many gravity items.

### EP-M06 — Time-eliminated relation

`v^2 = u^2 + 2as`

**SEE:** a problem where distance and speed matter but time is absent.

**REALIZE:** the relation connects velocity change directly to displacement under constant acceleration.

**UNDERSTAND:** eliminate time algebraically; explain squared velocities; derive stopping-distance scaling; use for vertical maximum height.

Source links: Q15, Q18, Q21, Q25, Q36, Q46, Q49-Q50, Q54-Q55, Q57-Q58.

### EP-M07 — nth-second motion

Start with:

`s_n = S(n) - S(n-1)`

Only after derivation use:

`s_n = u + (a/2)(2n - 1)`

**SEE:** timeline highlighting only the nth one-second interval.

**REALIZE:** "distance in n seconds" is cumulative; "distance in the nth second" is an interval.

**UNDERSTAND:** derive by subtraction; connect to `1:3:5:...` from rest and to differences of square numbers.

Source links: Q16-Q17, Q20, Q32, Q34, Q59.

### EP-M08 — Gravity

`a = +/- g`

**SEE:** drop or throw chalk.

**REALIZE:** gravity is not a new family of equations; it is acceleration with a chosen sign convention.

**UNDERSTAND:** establish signs; show `v = 0` at the top while `a = -g`; connect ordinary kinematics and vertical motion.

Source links: Q35-Q62.

### EP-M09 — Maximum height

`H = u^2 / (2g)` when assumptions are satisfied.

**SEE:** upward trajectory with the top marked `v = 0`.

**REALIZE:** all upward velocity has been lost at the highest point, but gravity still acts.

**UNDERSTAND:** derive from `v^2 = u^2 + 2as`; show `H proportional to u^2` and `H proportional to 1/g`; Earth/Moon comparisons require a supplied/established gravity ratio.

Source links: Q36-Q38, Q45, Q49-Q50, Q55, Q57-Q58.

### EP-M10 — Graph translations

- slope of `x-t` = velocity
- slope of `v-t` = acceleration
- signed area under `v-t` = displacement

**SEE:** draw physical motion and graph together.

**REALIZE:** graph geometry is another language for the motion story.

**UNDERSTAND:** translate words <-> graph <-> equation; test single-valuedness; distinguish graph height, slope and area.

Source links: Q63-Q68.

## Required contrast lessons

1. Q8-type equal-time average vs Q9-type equal-distance average.
2. Stopping time vs stopping distance.
3. Total distance to time `n` vs distance during the nth second.
4. Same speed vs same velocity.
5. Highest point: `v = 0` but `a != 0`.
6. "Dropped from rest" vs "released from a moving balloon/carrier".
7. One-object gravity equation vs relative motion when both bodies share `g`.
