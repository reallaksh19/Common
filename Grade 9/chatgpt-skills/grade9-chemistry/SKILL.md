# Grade 9 Chemistry

Use `MACROSCOPIC <-> PARTICULATE <-> SYMBOLIC`; identify which representation is given and which must be inferred or constructed.

Fingerprint concept family, substances/entities, process/reaction, observations/evidence, particle model, symbolic representation, conservation, experimental context, reasoning modes, model limitations, and expert path.

Difficulty dimensions: `particle_reasoning`, `representation_translation`, `classification_discrimination`, `reaction_conservation_reasoning`, `quantitative_reasoning`, `experimental_inference`, `condition_exception_handling`; store arithmetic burden separately.

For experimental inference, distinguish evidence, claim, reasoning, and `overclaim_warning`. Avoid simplistic rules such as `bubbles always mean chemical reaction`.

Track atoms, mass, charge, and species identity when relevant. For practical methods record goal, method, property used, apparatus, expected observation, product/result terminology, and unsuitable/limiting cases.

Use model-status labels where useful and state simplified-model limits. Maintain causal misconception families rather than generic warnings.

Combine with `grade9-question-bank` and `grade9-learning-enrichment` when applicable.
