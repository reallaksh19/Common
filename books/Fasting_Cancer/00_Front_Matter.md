## Note About the Data in This Book

Data described in this book have been obtained from currently ongoing animal or clinical studies. Therefore, beginning cycles of a fasting-mimicking diet is advised *only* after evaluation by and under the careful supervision of a patient’s own specialized doctor and oncologist.

It is extremely important to avoid malnutrition, a negative prognostic factor in both acute and chronic disease, but also to ensure that this and other nutritional interventions are appropriate considering the type and stage of cancer and the therapy to be administered (see Appendix 1).
