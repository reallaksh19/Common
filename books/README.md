# Books Library: Fasting, Nutrition, Longevity & Metabolic Health

This directory contains complete, chapterwise Markdown editions of foundational books on fasting, cellular rejuvenation, oncology nutrition, longevity science, and low-carb metabolic health.

Each book includes individual chapter files formatted for clean readability, complete consolidated single-file editions, structured tables of contents, scientific figures, and references.

---

## Catalog

### 1. [Fasting Cancer: How Fasting & Nutritechnology Are Creating Revolution in Cancer Prevention & Treatment](Fasting_Cancer/README.md)
- **Author:** Valter Longo, PhD (Director of the Longevity Institute at USC)
- **Publisher:** Avery / Penguin Random House (2025)
- **Scope:** 23 chapters / sections covering fasting-mimicking diets (FMD), differential stress resistance, cancer prevention, organ-specific oncology nutrition (breast, prostate, colorectal, lung, hematological, brain, skin, kidney), and clinical protocols.
- **Formats:**
  - [Chapterwise Directory](Fasting_Cancer/) (23 individual chapter files)
  - [Consolidated Edition](Fasting_Cancer/Fasting_Cancer_Complete_Chapterwise.md) (517k characters)

### 2. [The Longevity Diet: Discover the New Science Behind Stem Cell Activation and Regeneration](The_Longevity_Diet/README.md)
- **Author:** Valter Longo, PhD
- **Publisher:** Penguin Random House (2018)
- **Scope:** 20 chapters / sections covering the Five Pillars of Longevity, the everyday Longevity Diet, 5-day Fasting-Mimicking Diet (FMD) cycles, cellular reprogramming, disease prevention (cardiovascular, diabetes, neurodegenerative, autoimmune), and 2-week meal plans.
- **Formats:**
  - [Chapterwise Directory](The_Longevity_Diet/) (20 individual chapter files)
  - [Consolidated Edition](The_Longevity_Diet/The_Longevity_Diet_Complete_Chapterwise.md) (465k characters)
  - [Audit & Diff Report](The_Longevity_Diet/AUDIT_DIFF_REPORT.md) (Comparison between the 268-page unabridged edition and the 109-page audiobook companion booklet)

### 3. [Eat Rich, Live Long: Mastering the Low-Carb & Ketogenic Diet for Maximum Health and Longevity](Eat_Rich_Live_Long/README.md)
- **Authors:** Ivor Cummins & Jeffry Gerber, MD
- **Publisher:** Victory Belt Publishing (2018)
- **Scope:** 31 chapters / sections exploring hyperinsulinemia, the Kraft insulin assay, coronary artery calcium (CAC) scoring, lipid mechanics, low-carb / keto protocols (Days 1–7 and Days 8–21), a 51-recipe collection with full nutritional breakdowns and photography, and deep-dive appendices on cholesterol, insulin spectrums, and polyunsaturated fats (PUFAs).
- **Formats:**
  - [Chapterwise Directory](Eat_Rich_Live_Long/) (31 individual chapter files)
  - [Consolidated Edition](Eat_Rich_Live_Long/Eat_Rich_Live_Long_Complete_Chapterwise.md) (791k characters)
  - [Figures & Photography](Eat_Rich_Live_Long/images/) (161 high-resolution scientific diagrams and recipe photos)
