# 7-Day Methionine-Conscious Vegetarian DFSP Nutrition Plan

> **Status:** Draft nutrition research plan for clinical review. This is not a DFSP treatment protocol and is not a fasting-mimicking diet.

## Core targets

- Eating window: **06:30–18:30**
- Meals: **3/day**
- Calories: **~2,250–2,350 kcal/day**
- Protein: **~79–84 g/day**
- Protein/kg at 65.2 kg: **~1.21–1.29 g/kg/day**
- Methionine: **~1.05–1.18 g/day** (modeled, not directly assayed)
- BCAA: **~12–13 g/day** (modeled)
- Fiber: **~45–55+ g/day**, increased gradually if current intake is lower
- No calories after **18:30**

The design priority is to preserve weight and lean mass while lowering methionine exposure mainly through protein-source selection rather than by lowering total protein.

Protein-source hierarchy used in this plan:

**masoor/lentils → moong → chickpeas → moderate tofu → rajma → small dairy portions**

Calories are supplied mainly by whole grains, extra-virgin olive oil, walnuts/almonds, and moderate dairy fat rather than by excessive protein.

## Preparation rules

For moong, masoor, chickpeas and rajma:

1. Sort and rinse.
2. Soak where appropriate.
3. Discard soaking water.
4. Rinse again.
5. Pressure-cook thoroughly in fresh water.
6. Never consume undercooked kidney beans/rajma.
7. Avoid deep-frying, pan-frying, grilling and roasting as the main preparation.

Use approximately **3 g iodized salt/day total**, divided between meals, unless a treating clinician recommends otherwise.

All grain and pulse weights below are **cooked weights** unless dry weight is specifically stated.

# Special ingredient limits

## Pınar Labneh

Use approximately **40 g per serving, 3–4 days/week**, rather than a full 180 g tub at once.

Approximate modeled 40 g serving:

- ~78 kcal
- ~1.8 g protein
- ~54 mg methionine
- ~0.4 g BCAA

Pınar reports 4.5 g protein/100 g but does not publish an amino-acid profile, so methionine/BCAA values are estimated from comparable dairy protein profiles.

## Hung curd

Use approximately **50 g per serving** on selected days.

A Greek-yogurt/hung-curd proxy at 50 g contributes roughly:

- ~5 g protein
- ~78 mg methionine
- ~0.62 g BCAA

Exact values depend on preparation and degree of straining.

## Flaxseed

Use **5 g freshly ground/day**.

Approximate 5 g contribution:

- ~27 kcal
- ~0.9 g protein
- ~19 mg methionine
- ~0.16 g BCAA

Flax is included in a small amount, not used as a major protein source.

## Basil/sabja seed

Do not use sabja as a protein source because reliable whole-seed amino-acid data are insufficient for precise methionine/BCAA modeling.

If desired for fiber/texture, use about **5 g soaked sabja inside the eating window** and mark its sulfur-amino-acid contribution as not reliably quantified.

# Coffee, cacao and iron timing

- Optional black coffee: **1 cup after breakfast** if tolerated.
- Keep coffee/tea approximately **1–2 hours away from iron-focused pulse meals**, particularly if iron deficiency is confirmed.
- Optional unsweetened cacao: **5 g with breakfast**.
- Do not treat coffee, cacao, herbs or spices as anticancer therapy.

# Amla

Use **20–30 g fresh amla** with or shortly after a pulse-rich meal. This is a food-level vitamin-C strategy that may improve non-heme iron absorption.

---

# 12-hour fasting window

**18:30–06:30**

During the overnight fasting period:

- Water: yes
- Unsweetened noncaloric herbal tea: yes
- Black coffee: unnecessary at night
- Chia/sabja: no; these contain calories
- Labneh/hung curd: no
- Protein/BCAA/EAA drinks: no
- Caloric electrolyte drinks: no
- Psyllium: preferably before 18:30

No calories are required overnight for this plan.

---

# Exercise timing

If medically cleared, place resistance exercise approximately **16:00–17:00**, followed by the 17:30 meal.

Do not add isolated leucine or BCAA supplements routinely.

---

# Foods intentionally minimized under the methionine-conscious objective

- hard cheese
- large paneer servings
- large labneh servings
- whey/casein powders
- protein isolates
- BCAA/leucine powders
- sesame as a major protein source
- chia as a major protein source
- large pumpkin/sunflower seed protein loads

The rationale is lower protein efficiency for methionine exposure compared with lentils/moong/chickpeas.

---

# Monitoring and stop rules

Because measured body-fat mass is only about **3.1 kg**, maintaining nutritional reserve takes priority over methionine reduction.

Track body weight under similar conditions **2–3 times/week**.

If average weight falls by more than approximately **0.5 kg in one week**:

1. increase intake by roughly **200–300 kcal/day**, initially from calorie-dense foods such as EVOO, walnuts/almonds, or additional whole grain;
2. reassess appetite, GI tolerance and total intake;
3. abandon further methionine restriction if weight continues to fall.

Do not reduce toward 0.8 g/kg protein and do not attempt a 5–7-day fasting-mimicking or water-only fast while nutritional and laboratory issues remain unresolved.

# Evidence interpretation

This plan intentionally separates evidence levels:

- **DFSP HUMAN DATA:** supports PDGFB/PDGFRβ biology and downstream Akt/mTOR relevance, but not a therapeutic dietary methionine/BCAA target.
- **OTHER CANCER HUMAN DATA — EXTRAPOLATION:** severe methionine-restriction studies show systemic metabolic changes but are small and are not DFSP treatment evidence.
- **GENERAL ONCOLOGY NUTRITION:** supports maintaining adequate energy and protein and avoiding weight/muscle loss.
- **GENERAL NUTRITION EVIDENCE:** supports legumes, whole grains, vegetables, fiber and appropriate food preparation.

There is currently **no human DFSP evidence** that lowering dietary methionine, BCAA, leucine, glucose or total protein improves tumor control.

The target of ~1.05–1.18 g methionine/day is therefore a conservative food-selection experiment, not an established anticancer dose.

## Clinical-use note

This document is intended for review with the treating oncology team and an oncology dietitian. Nutritional adequacy, surgical readiness, anemia evaluation and maintenance of body mass take precedence over theoretical tumor-metabolism manipulation.
